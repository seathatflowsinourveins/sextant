# Paper lane

**Charter.** The proving ground: recorded fills on real broker paper accounts earn merit, and paper is never gated by P&L, loss budgets or trade counts. Its hard lines still bind — custody, one writer per account, the pacer, broker limits and orders, the owner halt, no live leakage, Decimal money. ([ADR-0002](../../docs/adr/0002-philosophy-and-lanes.md), [ADR-0007](../../docs/adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md))

**Hours.** 24/5 on the XNYS calendar with actual early closes: pre 04:00–09:30, regular, post to 20:00 and overnight 20:00–04:00 ET. Outside regular hours orders are LIMIT-only, and A's tiers trade at half risk.

**No conservative profile.** The owner, 2026-11-06: "no convervative in paper but full factors test e2e" ([ADR-0002](../../docs/adr/0002-philosophy-and-lanes.md), Changes for Sextant). That supersedes C's 0.25%, 1×, 20-name shape. Paper now tests the full factor stack end to end, and each factor set is carried as a paired with/without arm so its contribution is attributable; paper's hard lines and the rule that P&L alone decides nothing are unchanged. No replacement risk, leverage or name count is set for C — the lead recommends A's 1.0%, recorded in `docs/design.md` §What is not settled, not applied here.

**What it runs.** Every arm runs the whole stack — ranking generation, first leg, extension and reclaim, entry sizing, gain lock, breach and chase, backstop, take-profit roll, re-entry and rotation, and the catalyst screen — and each factor set is paired with an otherwise identical arm under the same fill, fee and latency models and the same `random_seed` ([ADR-0015](../../docs/adr/0015-research-lane-and-strategy-sourcing.md), [ADR-0018](../../docs/adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md)). All arms feed the one trial ledger; no tier is pooled and no history resets. The generic exit engine is ADR-0005/0025; B retains its separately scoped policy below, re-entry ADR-0006, guard and pacer ADR-0010, with house `client_order_id` lineage and one exit per quantity. IBKR paper exists to show the strategy is not Alpaca-shaped, not to add a second writer.

**C baselines.** C loads one baseline table per segment — overnight, pre, rth and post — from its baseline directory, and a missing file refuses that segment. Build all four from one tracked run: `python -m sextant.data.baseline_recipe --symbols AAPL MF --out DIR`, or `python -m sextant.data.baseline_recipe --universe DIR/universe.json --out DIR` where `DIR/universe.json` is the K2 ranked artifact written by `python -m sextant.paper.c_universe --out PATH`; the recipe never re-ranks. The run fetches overnight from the boats feed and pre/rth/post from sip through the reviewed fetchers, writes one segment per file with the baseline schema, and writes `manifest.json` last with the command, `as_of`, symbols, window, feeds and each file's sha256. Symbols are bare vendor tickers; a venue-qualified instrument ID such as `AAPL.ALPACA` is refused before any request. A segment that is zero across its window, or thinner than the schema floor, is named under `zero_filled` or `sparse` in the manifest and the run exits non-zero, so a zero-filled file cannot pass as a baseline. For arming, `sextant.ranking.baseline_coverage` separates the symbols the segment's table covers from those it does not; the missing names are recorded per symbol as `baseline_missing` and must not be armed.

**Negative evidence.** On 1,025 October 26–28 entries at assumed 3/6 bps per side, Hold 15:55 returned 76.44/70.42 bps versus the best active exit's 54.78/48.76; it beat all 96 active variants. The later six-arm minute approximation tied at −$55.14 gross and −$62.67 modelled net on two opportunities per arm, with zero qualified calibrated tick opportunities. Neither result validates gain protection or re-entry; retain the policy as a requirement and test its economics ([ADR-0025](../../docs/adr/0025-gain-protection-and-fast-exit.md)).

**Owner entries on paper B.** The owner places entries directly through TradingView's own Alpaca paper connection or by hand; no house tool relays them. The manager uses `external_order_instrument_ids` and `filter_unclaimed_external_orders=True` and protects every unreserved whole long share with exactly one house trail of the full net quantity, re-armed whenever that quantity changes. B is the sole house writer for its account and claims only positively reconciled owner fills; owner orders remain untouched. B retains its separately scoped policy: in RTH, the native trail rests until the node's bid trigger cancels it, waits for terminal cancellation, submits one collared/chased LIMIT, and re-arms any unfilled residual after reconciliation. Outside RTH, the entrypoint polls Alpaca's REST latest quote and latest trade for the held symbols only, SIP in pre/post and BOATS overnight, and the emulated BID_ASK trail reports each held symbol armed while the last successful poll is inside the registry `feed_silence_s` and that symbol has a quote; a standing quote stays armed through a quiet market, and the line reports both the poll's age and the quote's own age. A release is priced under that same condition, so a standing quote, or one stamped after the breach print, prices the LIMIT. A stopped poll, or a held symbol with no quote while polls succeed, pages `outside_rth_feed_silent` and reports `protected=0`; observations stamped before the session opened are rejected, the breach run restarts at each session/feed boundary, and the native trail's stop is adopted before the first quote of a new session. This policy and its required tests/review are not proof of installation or continuous protection. Under X2's adoption rule, entry remains the true cost basis, H starts at the adoption mark, and an earlier higher high requires an explicit labelled owner election; never substitute H = fill price or rebase entry to the mark ([ADR-0007](../../docs/adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md), [ADR-0025](../../docs/adr/0025-gain-protection-and-fast-exit.md), [ADR-0005](../../docs/adr/0005-exit-engine.md)). A restart is not an adoption; an owner add is ([ADR-0005](../../docs/adr/0005-exit-engine.md)). An owner add to a managed symbol re-arms the house trail to the full net quantity; owner order events that are not fills never freeze it, and pages are informational ([ADR-0007](../../docs/adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md)).

**What to place.** A plain DAY LIMIT buy: no bracket, no OCO, no trailing leg. A bracket's stop is a separate owner order the manager cannot adopt, adjust or share quantity with safely; the old paper records saw a take-profit cancel its stop in 15 of 15 observations; Alpaca brackets do not cover extended hours; and a native trailing stop would be a second full-size exit the manager must not run beside its own ([ADR-0005](../../docs/adr/0005-exit-engine.md), [ADR-0026](../../docs/adr/0026-tradingview-sync-and-order-presets.md)).

**Observed evidence.** The B export contains 162 filled-order summaries across 31 symbols: 118 labelled owner, 35 node and 9 coordinator-autoprotect, yielding 97 matched FIFO lots. It spans 2026-10-29 02:36:48.401546Z to 2026-11-03 15:48:12.528314Z; the first fill is October 28 ET. Recorded gross is −$15,372.014069821952. This is descriptive, unpaired evidence, not proof of claim latency, custody coverage or operation of the current exit policy. Separately, the PLTG broker witness records 5,555 shares bought at 16.47, trailing HWM 16.92, stop 16.497 and fill 16.36, losing $611.05 gross; it proves that an above-entry trigger did not guarantee a profitable fill ([ADR-0025](../../docs/adr/0025-gain-protection-and-fast-exit.md)).

**What is not proven.** Claim latency for a TradingView-originated fill; protection across the cancel/rewrite handovers, where B's cancel-first option has two unbounded gaps — between the cancel acknowledgement and the LIMIT acknowledgement, and between that LIMIT's acceptance and its fill or reconciliation; B's gain-protection economics; and any live acceptance. The pinned deployment also still starts 24 rows at 0.03 while the newer ruling caps B at 0.025 ([ADR-0025](../../docs/adr/0025-gain-protection-and-fast-exit.md)).

**Session practice.** After each completed session, write one `docs/research/YYYY-MM-DD-paper-session.md` from read-only outputs. It never takes an operational action.
- Every number cites the exact command that produced it, its input or report, and the covered interval; a non-numeric claim cites its source too.
- Anything not observed is **unknown**, never 0 and never omitted; an observed zero is valid only with its producing command.
- Report expected versus measured per deployment and active-or-retired, with fills, slippage against model, guard events and budget use.
- Compare A/B on normalized returns, never dollar P&L, with the capital denominator and cash-flow treatment named; a session return is not proof of superiority.
- State the day-roll boundary (20:00 America/New_York), the covered interval and the evidence it has ended before writing; if the roll is unconfirmed, do not write a premature note.
- Propose memories as proposals only, with evidence; no worker writes a memory store.

**Promotion.** Recorded fills reach merit at ≥30 independent round trips over ≥5 sessions with a measured minimum track record at PSR ≥ 0.95, replay correlation ≥0.9, slippage ≤1.5× modelled and no unexplained risk event; merit produces a Stage-0 draft, never a promotion ([ADR-0019](../../docs/adr/0019-validation-and-merit.md)). Only the lead promotes, with a record.
