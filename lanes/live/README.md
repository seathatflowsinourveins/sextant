# Live lane

**Charter.** The only place Sextant can reach a broker with real money, and only through one path: a merged `live` branch, a merge commit, the `promotion` record and `deployments/live.json`, run by the `live` Environment job under its own OS user. Agents never touch a live endpoint or a live credential. The lead promotes, stages and demotes with a record; the owner stores the keys, approves and applies the limits, and can halt. ([ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-live-lane.md), [ADR-0001](../../docs/adr/0001-sextant-foundation.md))

**Hours.** Initial strategies enter only in regular hours, stop new entries at close −30 minutes and are flat by close −15; no overnight. A wider session needs its own promotion record.

**Limits.** Owner ceiling 1,000 requests/min; 870 configured only after a written entitlement confirmation, and above 1,000 needs FIX. Stage 1 runs at most 30 actions/min with a node ceiling of 164 HTTP attempts/min, reserving 36 for halts; the conditional later ceiling of 964 needs measured entitlement. The approved baseline policy is L ≤ 1, entry ≤ 2% of equity, symbol ≤ 5%, strategy gross ≤ 10%, at most 5 positions, with latches at 1% daily, 3% weekly and 10% drawdown, and the engine's own all-order cap above the position ceiling. Higher leverage tiers are OFF. ([ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-live-lane.md), [ADR-0009](../../docs/adr/0009-order-rate-limits.md))

**Stages.** 0 shadow, 1 fill realism, 2 and 3 size steps, each with its own recorded evidence: shadow needs two sessions of every traded type, 20 intended orders, parity ≥ 0.95 and timing p95 ≤ 2 s; fill realism needs 100 live fills and 30 round trips over three sessions with `D = P&L_live − P&L_paper`, a 95% interval above −0.5 × modelled edge, total cost ≤ 1.5× and drawdown ≤ 1.5× backtest; a size step needs 50 fills at the new size over two sessions. A halt, a missing review or fidelity below 0.9 sends it back to Stage 0. ([ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-live-lane.md))

**What it runs.** Only a recorded promotion under ADR-0020 may run. Carry [northstar live contract](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/northstar/blob/b384d298d217d3f8d9aa83356994629536a34795/docs/decisions/0053-live-lane-automated-evidence-gated.md) and both [protective-exit](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/northstar/blob/b384d298d217d3f8d9aa83356994629536a34795/docs/decisions/amendments/0053-2026-10-31-protective-exits.md) and [owner-cover](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/northstar/blob/b384d298d217d3f8d9aa83356994629536a34795/docs/decisions/amendments/0053-2026-10-31-protective-exits-owner-cover.md) amendments in full by pinned reference; the summary here waives none of their gates. Sextant substitutes only exact-SHA two-family review for the inherited live verdict. Stage 2 requires NautilusTrader 2.0.0 final. Live retains its separately sanctioned protective policy; paper B's cancel-first exception is not automatically promoted. Owner-exit management requires its bounded mandate and proved handback before leg expiry, and remains refused until those conditions are met. Implementation and readiness are unverified ([ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-live-lane.md)).

**Halt.** `scripts/live.sh halt [--flatten]` persists the cause, stops entries and increasing orders, and cancels through the pacer; a supervisor takes over after 10 seconds without a node acknowledgement. A healthy plain halt keeps protection resident; a flatten uses priced, sliced, paced marketable limits with terminal proof. An unknown state stays halted until positive terminal proof for every possible fill, and no restart, reconnect or marker deletion clears a cause. ([ADR-0020](../../docs/adr/0020-live-lane.md))

**Rules.**
- Never widen a limit, never improvise an order, never skip a drill. Promotion is the only door.
- The owner's orders are untouched; an owner-plan lane needs a bounded mandate and a dedicated account ([ADR-0007](../../docs/adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md)).
- One writer per account, one exit per quantity, and no second full-size exit while protection is resident.
- Every order, fill and halt reconciles to the append-only audit record; an unexplained broker order halts entries.
