## The life of a trade

**1. Rank.** Score each active NMS common stock, NMS ADR and TXSE name — every market cap, sub-$1 included — by `move × dvol_ratio` against its own time-matched segment baseline, ties by `trades_accel`; `move` is measured from the latest completed split-adjusted official regular-session close, and dollar volume never enters the score. Recompute on every eligible trade, publish at most once per `publish_min_interval_s`, and treat a missing, stale or invalid generation as no entry ([ADR-0003](adr/0003-ranking-and-feeds.md)). Historical scanner evidence, not a K2 validation: at the earliest saved publication on 2026-10-27, 04:49:39 ET, recall within the top 30 was 6/41 (14.6341%) and within the top 100 was 25/41 (60.9756%). Truth was the saved, coverage-conditional positive prior-close gainers with observed premarket dollars above $1 million, not a +5% cohort. The partial replay changed recall by zero percentage points. At 07:00 the corresponding counts were 10/50 and 28/50; at 09:00, 4/50 and 15/50. These observations establish neither full-market recall nor the K2 score's after-cost edge ([evidence](research/2026-11-06-extreme-mover-evidence.md)). Unmeasured: the after-cost edge of any threshold, and any multi-year hit rate. The owner's package also holds a historical 31-factor composite and its top 100 selected gainers, distinct from this production rank formula. Its held-out AUC is 0.986 prior / 0.989 fitted and its top-100 T+20 median is −6.22% on only 55 observed returns versus −12.76% on 627 scored-gainer outcomes; these are selected-event and post-spike descriptions. Selected losers supply the other AUC class, ordinary non-movers are absent, and RS/13D backfill is gainer-only. These results cannot establish ranking every stock before it moves; the fitted weights remain a proposal ([ADR-0027](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/blob/e08550fa73cc72a66373eb303ecb0a10ebd1b74d/docs/adr/0027-extreme-gainer-research-package.md), [package evidence](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/blob/e08550fa73cc72a66373eb303ecb0a10ebd1b74d/docs/research/2026-11-06-extreme-gainer-package.md)).

**2. Enter early.** The primary setup is the first leg: within `first_leg_window_s` of a surge, a non-decreasing run of `first_leg_trades_count` eligible trades spanning `first_leg_span_s`, no more than `first_leg_max_move` above the quiet price; a lower trade restarts the run. A breakout needs a fresh `breakout_lookback` high with covered flow; an extended spike is entered only on pullback, reclaim and a held higher-low retest, never at the high. EXTENDED when `last − segment VWAP > entry_max_vwap_extension_atr × ATR`, and a missing ATR or VWAP is UNKNOWN, never "not extended" ([ADR-0004](adr/0004-entry.md)). Orders are LIMIT at the ask, `extended_hours` outside RTH with `time_in_force` `day` or `gtc` ([ADR-0029](adr/0029-second-family-corrections.md)), repriced at most `entry_chase_bps`, cancelled after `entry_ttl_s`, never market. Unmeasured: whether this beats a plain open-range entry after costs; the sub-5% trigger grid is an uncalibrated candidate, not a default.

**3. Size from risk.** Freeze `bp_target_fraction × buying power` at segment start, then `stop_distance = entry − initial_trigger + chase_cap_fraction × entry` and planned dollars `= basis × stop_ref_fraction ÷ portfolio_slots × stopout_multiplier × confidence_multiplier`, bounded by the smaller of uncommitted buying power and `flow_share_cap ×` recent dollar volume; no read of buying power means no entry ([ADR-0004](adr/0004-entry.md), [ADR-0011](adr/0011-sizing-and-auto-leverage.md)). A keeps mover slots `ceil(0.4 × cap)` (2 of 5) and holds the top `3 ×` that quota in the candidate pool; the 0.4 split is a starting value, not an optimum. Unmeasured: every sizing multiplier and that share ([ADR-0024](adr/0024-extreme-mover-ranking-and-early-entry.md)).

**4. Protect the gain.** With `H` the eligible running high since entry, `buffer = max(one_tick, spread, prearm_buffer_fraction × entry, prearm_range_multiple × R_w)`, `gain_lock = max(entry + c_RT, H − max(noise_floor, gain_lock_giveback_fraction × (H − entry)))`, and the sell trigger `max(previous_trigger, H − max(buffer, setup_gap), gain_lock when armed)`: it starts below entry and only rises ([ADR-0005](adr/0005-exit-engine.md), [ADR-0025](adr/0025-gain-protection-and-fast-exit.md)). Arming needs a completed minute close at or above `entry + c_RT + noise_floor` and an executable price strictly above the armed trigger. Every in-gain holding, including the owner's own entries on B, carries broker-held protection that follows the gain — a replaced OCO/bracket `stop_price`, or one native trailing stop for stop-only custody — because the owner found the old lane's auto gain protection was not executed ([ADR-0025](adr/0025-gain-protection-and-fast-exit.md)). Unmeasured: that this protection adds basis points; its values need cited methods, not invented thresholds.

B is the sole house writer for its account and claims only positively reconciled owner fills; owner orders remain untouched. B retains its separately scoped policy: in RTH, the native trail rests until the node's bid trigger cancels it, waits for terminal cancellation, submits one collared/chased LIMIT, and re-arms any unfilled residual after reconciliation. Outside RTH, its emulated BID_ASK trailing stop-limit depends on the node and feed. This policy and its required tests/review are not proof of installation or continuous protection. Under X2's adoption rule, entry remains the true cost basis, H starts at the adoption mark, and an earlier higher high requires an explicit labelled owner election; never substitute H = fill price or rebase entry to the mark ([ADR-0007](adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md), [ADR-0025](adr/0025-gain-protection-and-fast-exit.md), [ADR-0005](adr/0005-exit-engine.md)).

**5. Sell fast on the first non-noise sell.** A breach is `breach_persist_count` consecutive eligible bids (RTH) or trades (outside) at or below the trigger over at least `breach_persist_span_s`; one quote, print or bar never sells, and the 5-minute rotation cadence is not an exit clock. On breach, amend the one managed LIMIT to `max(bid, last × (1 − chase_cap_fraction))`, at most once per `chase_min_interval_s`, serialized, with acknowledgement treated as no fill; a native stop leg is raised in place to `max(previous_backstop, min(trigger, bid) × (1 − backstop_min_separation_fraction))` and never lowered ([ADR-0005](adr/0005-exit-engine.md)). Finite take-profits roll above `H` by `take_profit_above_h_fraction` and step only when `H` rises `take_profit_step_fraction`, so a pending roll can cap a runner. On 1,025 complete-horizon strategy entries from 2026-10-26–28, the minute-bar study's Hold 15:55 control returned 76.44/70.42 bps per entry at assumed 3/6 bps per side, beating all 96 active variants; the best active variant returned 54.78/48.76. Its market-state overlay lost 38.09/38.08 bps against the otherwise identical OFF arm, and 87/96 variants had nonpositive stressed expectancy. This was not a gain-lock ablation, tick-execution test or WF/CPCV/DSR/PBO validation. Separately, the later six-arm strict-protection study had zero qualified cost-calibrated tick opportunities and no hold-flat control. Keep the owner's policy; the research/execution leads settle its economics with the preregistered executable cohort and full trial accounting ([evidence](research/2026-11-06-extreme-mover-evidence.md)).

**6. Re-enter or rotate.** Only after a flat receipt: broker net quantity zero plus terminal lineage for the entry and every protection. Then wait `cooldown_seconds`, retry the same symbol while its rank stays inside `retry_rank_band`, or rotate to the best unheld qualified name inside `rotation_rank_band`; a reclaim needs a pullback, a held higher low and a hold, buys at most `reclaim_max_above_buffers` buffers above the level and never the first print. Held rotation needs `expected_lift > 2 × c_RT ÷ price + rotation_lift_margin_bps ÷ 10,000` ([ADR-0006](adr/0006-reentry-and-rotation.md)). Paper retries are unbounded and ungated; live is `live_nonrenewing`, 1×, with retry and rotation OFF until a separate promotion. Unmeasured: whether re-entry recovers more than it costs, which is why paper proves it first.

## Portfolio, sizing and auto-leverage

Sizing is full-portfolio equal planned risk through `FixedRiskSizer`, with every order inside fresh buying power and a flow-share cap; effective leverage is an output, never an input, and a challenger never vetoes the incumbent ([ADR-0011](adr/0011-sizing-and-auto-leverage.md)). Allocation is skfolio 1.12.5 HRP with Ledoit–Wolf covariance on completed daily paper history, computed offline at the session boundary, one joint covariance, Decimal money, one atomic generation; never cumulative dollars as a percentage, never an invented zero correlation ([ADR-0012](adr/0012-portfolio-management.md)).

The sizing challenger uses λ_dd = clip((E − α·HWM) / ((1 − α)·E), 0, 1) and B = E − α·HWM; α = 0.85 is an uncalibrated, unverified start. It sizes nothing without a MEASURED arm × price-band × session cell and measured gap input; adoption requires the recorded paired stressed-cost gates. Separately, fixed guard mode uses min(configured, L_dd, L_broker), but paper observes and reports L_dd without applying it. Policy de-levering reduces new entries rather than forcing sales, and operational recovery never resets the economic HWM. Live remains 1× pending its full gates and the owner's concrete higher limits ([ADR-0011](adr/0011-sizing-and-auto-leverage.md)).

## Execution

**Custody.** The house `client_order_id` plus a durable replacement lineage is provenance; the broker's strategy tag is not. The owner's orders are never cancelled, sold back, resized or adopted; a report grants no custody; every unreserved whole long share is covered by exactly one house trail of the full net quantity, re-armed (cancel plus one resubmit) whenever that quantity changes, while owner order events that are not fills never freeze the symbol and pages are informational. An unlinked owner LIMIT reserves quantity, so only the unreserved residual is protected; a fully reserved position raises `owner_reserved_unprotected` instead of blocking ([ADR-0007](adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md)).

**One writer, one pacer.** Exactly one writer and one pacer per broker account, one exit per quantity, never a second full-size exit; foreign open orders or a quantity mismatch freeze the symbol and page. Orders are built only by the runtime, from intents emitted by pure modules, through the pacer and a synchronous reducer; acknowledgement is not a fill and flat needs a broker-flat receipt plus terminal lineage ([ADR-0007](adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md), [ADR-0009](adr/0009-order-rate-limits.md)). Pacing counts every wire action, prioritizes CANCEL > REDUCE > MODIFY > QUERY > SUBMIT, reserves protection capacity per open and exiting position and keeps the broker risk engine enabled ([ADR-0007](adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md), [ADR-0010](adr/0010-risk-guard-pacing-and-halt.md)).

**Guard and halt.** The `RiskGuardActor` persists its kill marker before fallible work, cancels entries through their owners, allows a 10-second cancel window, then reduces until broker net is flat; paper observes these gates and live enforces them, with an unknown gate identifier enforcing and logging an error. Halt is `scripts/live.sh halt [--flatten]`: it stops entries and increasing orders, a supervisor takes over after 10 seconds without a node acknowledgement, protection is retained on a plain halt, and an unexplained or unknown state stays halted until positive terminal proof ([ADR-0010](adr/0010-risk-guard-pacing-and-halt.md), [ADR-0020](adr/0020-live-lane.md)).

## Compounding learning

Every exit and incident leaves a `lesson/1` record — one schema, 15 required fields, append-only, deduplicated by `(area, subject, session_date, sha256(lesson))`, corrections as new records that supersede, negative results kept — written through one appender whose writer is the Alpaca lane and whose provenance is the canonical handbook, never a narrative memory ([ADR-0018](adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md)).

Each tunable value has a registry row and source. A sourced value without calibration is an uncalibrated starting value, never measured success. If the value or its authority is missing, keep it unavailable; fixtures supply no default authority. Reviewed changes occur between sessions, and nightly tuning only proposes. Every output carries a `params_hash` ([ADR-0018](adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md)).

The [L4 nightly proposal design](research/2026-11-06-nightly-proposals.md) separates the recorded-evidence candidate job from reflection and adoption: no registry patch or canonical append, even through a reviewed-write callback.
