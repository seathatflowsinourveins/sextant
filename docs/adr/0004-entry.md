# ADR-0004: Enter the first rising leg and size every entry from its risk

- **Status:** accepted. Harvested; the decision is unchanged unless "Changes for Sextant" says otherwise.
- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Harvested from:** northstar-lanes@00203275944a:docs/adr/0005-entry.md, whole record and amendments.

## Context
An early, qualified US equity entry must remain protectable within the order budget.
Buying an extended spike or sizing from dwindling buying power defeats that purpose.

## Decision
- **Setups (N1):** first leg is primary: inside `first_leg_window_s` of a surge or catalyst plus surge, at most `first_leg_max_move` above quiet price, require `first_leg_trades_count` non-decreasing eligible trades spanning `first_leg_span_s`, above quiet. A lower trade restarts the run. `above_start` is shadow only. Breakouts need a fresh `breakout_lookback` high, covered flow and NOT_EXTENDED; accumulation needs its flag, first rising second, cap above base high and NOT_EXTENDED.
- **Covered flow:** eligible trades over `covered_flow_window_s` at least `covered_flow_ratio` times the average rate over `covered_flow_baseline_min`. Read rising and flow on live eligible trades at the decision; an earlier rank never substitutes.
- **Fresh reclaim:** an extension episode ends at its first later eligible touch at/below segment VWAP. That touch must belong to this episode and fall within `reclaim_lookback_s`; require consecutive reclaim trades and span above the level. Buy at most `reclaim_max_above_buffers` buffers above it, in [0, 1], so the new trigger stays at/below the level.
- **Held retest:** after reclaim trades, require a pullback from their running high, a trough above the touch low and at/above the level, then `reclaim_hold_s` without a lower trade. Lower trades restart the hold; below-level trades void the setup until another touch; a new high before completion requires a new pullback. Buy on the first eligible trade above the held trough, within the cap. Never buy a reclaim's first print; `first_print` is shadow only.
- **Extension (N2):** EXTENDED iff `last − segment VWAP > entry_max_vwap_extension_atr × ATR`; an extended fresh entry needs reclaim. First leg and candidate HALT_RESUME have their own bounded windows/caps; OVERSELL has its flush-low stop. No setup buys an unbounded fresh spike high. Breakout/accumulation also require `max(last, ask) − VWAP ≤ entry_max_vwap_extension_atr × ATR`.
- Explicitly reset VWAP at every segment boundary. ATR needs `atr_period` completed `atr_bar_minutes` bars from that symbol. Missing ATR or VWAP is UNKNOWN, never NOT_EXTENDED; it permits no breakout/accumulation or extension episode, while bounded exempt setups keep their own rules.
- **Direction (N3):** paper entries are long only; no entry on a negative source-stated catalyst or a falling tape. HALT_RESUME remains a labelled candidate requiring confirmed halt/resume, a live score, rising flow, capped buy and its bounded extension exemption.
- **Orders (N4):** LIMIT at ask; `extended_hours` outside RTH, with `time_in_force` `day` or `gtc` ([ADR-0029](0029-second-family-corrections.md) C2). At most four decimals below $1.00 and two at/above it. Re-price no farther than `entry_chase_bps` above the first limit; cancel after `entry_ttl_s`. Never a market order; one writer per quantity.
- **Sizing basis (N5):** freeze `bp_target_fraction × TradeAccount.buying_power` at segment start, persist with segment identity and reuse on restart. A first read at node start is labelled; no successful read means no entry. Re-read uncommitted buying power each cycle only for hard limits and rotation, never as the planned-risk basis.
- **Risk:** use Nautilus `FixedRiskSizer`. `stop_distance = entry − initial_trigger + chase_cap_fraction × entry`, counting chase once; `stop_loss = entry − stop_distance`. Ordinary initial trigger is `entry − buffer_at_entry`; OVERSELL uses `L − buffer` and keeps that wider distance until gain lock arms. Planned dollars = `basis × stop_ref_fraction ÷ portfolio_slots × stopout_multiplier × confidence_multiplier`; pass planned dollars/equity as `risk`.
- Confidence multiplier = `confidence_size_floor + (1 − confidence_size_floor) × confidence`; scored OVERSELL/RECLAIM/BREAKOUT and ACCUMULATION use their own scores. First-leg/HALT_RESUME are labelled unscored: no multiplier, never invented confidence 1. Same-symbol re-entry without a score is refused. Stop-out multiplier is `stopout_size_decay` raised to consecutive pre-arm stop-outs, floored at `stopout_size_floor`; reset on armed exit or segment boundary.
- `hard_limit = min(uncommitted buying power, flow_share_cap × dollar volume over flow_share_window_s) ÷ entry`. Call the long sizer only with `entry > 0`, `equity > 0`, `0 < stop_loss < entry`, positive planned risk and `hard_limit ≥ 1 share`; quantity below one share means no order. Journal failing terms, planned/realized loss and the binding flow bound.
- **Portfolio (N6):** add highest-scored qualified candidates toward the deployment target using capacity left after protection reserve. No separate position cap. If buying power cannot fund the next candidate, it must score at least `rotation_score_ratio` times the weakest holding and meet held-rotation lift. Heat, gross/name/sector exposure, count and correlation are observe-only on paper; live uses approved limits. Pre-size and subscribe calendar catalysts before their window.
- **Eligibility (N7 as amended):** E1 price < $0.10 and E6 identity (common stock or labelled NMS ADR) apply at arming in both pools. E2 spread > 2% of mid and E3 last-5-minute dollar volume < $250k OR 20-day ADV$ < $1M are observations on paper and enforced on live. Paper entries proceed with spread/depth logged; promotion charges spread and square-root impact at 1x and 2x. E4 follows upstream venue halt/LULD state.
- E5 refuses positive evidence of recent offering, reverse split or extreme dilution; paper admits missing measurements with `e5_unverified`. Live enforces once the producer carries row-level clearance. Missing ADR catalyst coverage means "no catalyst". IBKR sub-$1 remains NONE at rc7 because its static `min_tick` lacks the $1 ladder.
- **Tier S:** otherwise eligible names; half lane risk, 1x, notional ≤ 5% of prior-5-minute dollar volume, R floor ≥ 3x live spread measured on c5 initial distance, and stops under 3%. On A/C, S may enter outside RTH with LIMIT orders wherever the broker/protection can hold it; other lanes retain their scoped limits. C trades every session, flat by actual session end minus five minutes, no boundary carry. Tier L is price ≥ $10 and ADV$ ≥ $50M. S-OFF is a reviewed proposal on after-cost expectancy below zero beyond paired noise, never a two-week calendar wait.
- **Mover-aware selection, A/C only:** caps A 5/C 20; mover quota `ceil(0.4 × cap)` = A 2/C 8, potential gets the rest. Custody counts first and is never dropped. Fill potential first, then admitted/qualified/unvetoed movers from the same fresh snapshot, inside/outside top-N; unused slots transfer immediately. Movers meet trigger gap and sort `move × rvol`, then RVOL, then symbol. RVOL temporarily stands for `dvol_ratio`; downstream setup/protection is unchanged.
- Journal pool/rank/move/RVOL/score/previous close; each refresh keeps top `3 × mover quota` (A 6/C 24); fills/TCA retain pool. The causal `mover-arming` sim has 48 cells plus 4 controls per stratum; never pool A/C. The 0.4 split is a starting value, not an optimum.
- **Calibration:** all numeric registry rows are UNCALIBRATED until measured. Below-5% triggers remain candidates, not deployment defaults. Six starts (`first_leg_trades_count`, `first_leg_span_s`, `first_leg_max_move`, `entry_max_vwap_extension_atr`, `entry_chase_bps`, `entry_ttl_s`) need a reviewed registry change; fixtures supply no authority. First-leg after-cost edge and missing witness inputs remain **unverified**. ORB is a negative replay diagnostic, never this primary allocator.
