## Decision

- **One path:** only `scripts/live.sh`, from merged/gated `live`, uses approved `configs/live/limits.yaml` and `instruments.yaml`, `deployments/live.json`, `reports/live/promotions/<strategy>-<date>.json` and append-only `reports/live/audit/<date>.jsonl`. Missing limits refuse boot (exit 4); never fall back to `limits.example.yaml`. Agents never directly touch live endpoints, credentials or live-config values. Lead promotes/stages/demotes; owner stores credentials, approves/applies limits and halts. [P]
- **Exact record:** bind strategy/policy/version, registry, dossier, every gate's value/report, exit-plan/hash, code V/verification, engine gate, kind, stage/history, fill-gap block, limits hash, approver and times. V is an ancestor of deployed head; only `deployments/live.json` and `reports/live/promotions/**` may differ. Promotion-only changes need their own review. Every order/fill/halt reconciles to its audit; an unexplained broker order halts entries. Alpaca client IDs are ≤128 characters; rc7 IB uses ClientOrderId→order_ref. [P]
- **2a, paper proven:** WF/CPCV pass; DSR >0.95 with effective N; PBO ≤0.05 (CSCV S=16); PSR ≥0.95; sign retained at 2× costs; untouched 12–24-month holdout. Independent round-trip MinTRL, ≥30 paper round trips over ≥5 sessions covering every traded type, replay correlation ≥0.9, slippage ≤1.5× modeled, no unexplained risk event and dossier. No calendar minimum. Merit yields a Stage-0 draft, never automatic admission. [G]
- **Nonwaived gates:** same-runner paper/sim drills cover over-limit refusal, owner/automatic halts and suspension effects, kill file, restart with positions, protective/time stops, flatten and audit; include outage, supervisor and C.5 double-exit drills. Scan universes need dated/delisted membership, `asof` on every request and `survivor_only:false`; fixed liquid lists declare selection date and survivorship bias. Feed acceptance includes first/final revisions, event/init skew, deterministic scanner and catalyst knowable-at audit. Hash the complete protective/time/holding/catalyst/re-entry exit plan. [G]
- **2b, owner plan:** a specific recorded owner instruction names instrument, size, entry and exit thesis; `kind:owner_plan` replaces only its named offline/paper-merit gates. Limits, clean identity/reconciliation, exact code/review, exit plan and stages remain. Mid-plan changes need a new recorded instruction; agents never widen a plan. Stage 0–1 may use rc7 with dated `engine_gate:owner_ack_rc`; Stage 2 requires NautilusTrader 2.0.0 final. rc8 needs its risk-release verification before adoption. [G]
- **Stage 0, shadow:** live feed/account observation and intended-order sink, zero submitted orders. ≥2 sessions of every traded type, ≥20 intended orders, parity ≥0.95, timing p95 ≤2 s, zero risk events, clean starts and feed-fidelity bounds. Later accepted Q1 uses the read-only recording sink **without broker suspension**, after T4 r2c-or-later no-submit proof and `sink.failed` latch fix with reviews; a halt/fidelity breach fixes and restarts evidence. Shadow protects nothing. [S, X]
- **Stage 1, fill realism:** ≥100 live fills, ≥30 round trips, ≥3 sessions, and `n_signals ≥ (1.96·σ_D/(0.25·E_model))²`. Pair every paper signal, including missed (live P&L=0), partial and unfinished outcomes; `D=P&L_live−P&L_paper` in intended-notional bps. Session-block-bootstrap 95% CI; mean live P&L/signal >0; `lower95(mean D)>−0.5·E_model`; `E_lower+mean D>0`; total cost ≤1.5× modeled; fill rate ≥0.9× paper; drawdown ≤1.5× backtest; zero unexplained halts. [S]
- **Stage 2–3:** double size within every cap only after ≥50 fills at new size over ≥2 sessions, new mean cost ≤previous upper95, continuing Stage-1 economics, drawdown ≤1.5× backtest and no halt. Target size keeps these forever. Demote to paper at ≥30 live trips for cost >1.5× modeled or fill ratio <0.75, or full-sample economic failure; write costs/gap back and rerun validation, re-entering at Stage 0. Any halt steps back; missing/FAIL review or fidelity <0.9 returns to Stage 0. [S]
- **Dynamic limits:** upper bounds are `min(ADR ceiling, fresh-input/exact-arm evidence formula)`; liquidity floors use max and categorical rules are predicates. Missing/stale/nonfinite/unbound inputs bar increases, preserving valid reductions/protection except a no-new-order cause. Size with `W_s=max(raw60,raw90)` for 90-minute coverage, ≥5 years of 1-minute RTH risk data and `N_s·W_s≤0.5·daily-loss-budget`; retain both horizons and provenance. The strict proposed shared increase-only contract is `Σ(N_s·W_s)+C_exit≤0.5·min(D,D_remaining)`, never an owner-exit admission gate. New-listing reference classes need research evidence and an authorized history switch. [L]
- **Approved baseline policy, not an enacted file:** L≤1; entry ≤2%E, symbol ≤5%E, strategy gross ≤10%E, ≤5 positions; engine all-order cap=1.5× effective position ceiling (≤7.5%E before tighter bounds), separate from entry cap. Daily/weekly/drawdown latches 1%/3%/10%; inherited outer ceilings remain 3%/6%/15%, entry 10%E, position 25%E, strategy 50%E, 50 positions, L 2.0 intraday/1.5 overnight, never activation authority. Initial strategies: RTH entries only, stop new entries close−30, flat close−15, no overnight. Price ≥$5, ADV ≥500,000; US common stocks/eligible ETFs on named venues. [L]
- **Higher tiers remain OFF:** T15 L≤1.5, T20 L≤2; entry/symbol/strategy ≤5%/10%/25%E and ≤20 positions; ≥2/≥3 unused distinct-root incentives respectively. U1–U6 require frozen identity/negative eligibility, matched-risk paired OOS lower95 incremental payoff >0 at 2× all costs, trial corrections, per-stratum MinTRL/≥30 trips/≥5 sessions, drills, final engine/protection, `gho` verdict/new record and exact promotion. T20 also needs T15's ≥50-fill/≥2-session size-step evidence. Step down to ≤1E close−30 and flat close−15. No paper multiplier or configured cap enables live leverage; shorting, 3×/inverse ETFs, options and OTC stay excluded; ≤2× daily-reset ETFs need their separate arm/holding record. [L]
- **Rate budgets:** Stage-1 Alpaca ≤30 actions/min and node ≤164 total HTTP attempts/min, all reads/pages/retries/lookups charged; reserve 36=13 quiet+23 headroom. Incident liveness ≤2/min leaves ≥34 for halt work, previously consumed attempts still charged. Conditional later node ≤964=1,000−36 needs measured live entitlement; paper stays 200 account requests/min. IB live ≤10 actions/s pending independent paper cancel/flatten proof. The record's reading is that 50 requests/s at 100 lines is a shared cap, not orders plus reads; the owner's 3,000/min is the separate order-action limit in [ADR-0009](0009-order-rate-limits.md). [L, K]
- **Halt:** `scripts/live.sh halt [--flatten]` persists kill/cause, stops entries and cancels increasing orders; supervisor takes over after 10 s without node acknowledgement. Healthy plain halt retains protection; heartbeat stale 60 s with positions escalates. Flatten uses paced, pre-named, price-capped/sliced marketable limits, backoff 1–60 s, positive broker terminal proof, then suspension LAST/readback. Alert after 5 min or 10 consecutive instrument failures. Only a separately sanctioned ONE-SHOT RTH last escalation may use native close-all; never auto-selected/retried. [K]
- **Unknowns stay halted:** fence former sender/emulator/rearm; no second closer without terminal proof for every possible-fill ID, complete orders/legs/replacements, fresh holdings and corporate-action reconciliation. Empty cache, timeout, quiet time or process exit is not success. The exact ConnectTimeout NOT_TRANSMITTED exception requires **all G1–G7** and identity transaction proof; otherwise UNKNOWN. Preserve monotonic 30-second unreachable halt, attempt aging, request deadlines and durable restart state; same-boot windows restore, new-boot reset needs proven identity. Quiet REST cadence is account/positions/orders/config every 10/15/30/60 s. Incident reads never contend ahead of halt. [K]
- **Release and reduction:** cancel entries/reservations then reduce strategy exposure largest first to compliance. Multiple causes compose most restrictively; plain owner halt needs fresh typed owner confirmation, automatic/daily-loss release needs recorded lead release and a later trading day. No restart/reconnect/marker deletion clears a cause. FAIL/missing running-code review, identity/config mismatch or unresolved reconciliation permits **no new orders**, including protective replacement. [K, X]
- **Owner exits:** `kind:owner_exit_policy` needs a dedicated owner account, bounded position mandates, exact policy/schema/evidence and policy-version parity from paper-B TCA/custody plus live shadow, never mirrored entries. Owner holdings/P&L count in common latches, but strategy concentration/RTH-flat rules and automatic liquidation do not apply to owner holdings. Owner `halt --flatten` covers **all** holdings. Manual cancel/replace revokes management until a new mandate. Cache-only `read-account` serves admitted T7 cache, adds no broker request and refuses during incidents; no worker socket authority. [X]
- **Latest protective policy:** managed dynamic LIMIT is primary, rolling TP above H plus coupled plain-stop backstop; both legs remain resident in steady state. Breach chase uses `max(bid,last·(1−chase_cap_fraction))`, never below bid; `k_chase` is halt/close only. Backstop freezes at last ACK in outage; Chandelier is counterfactual-only, not live trigger. Healthy permitted halt retains both legs/chasing; stop-only conversion needs owner election. Owner-position adoption is **REFUSED UP FRONT** until bounded handback before earliest leg expiry is proved; no timer-only/GTC assurance. Unsupported fractional or outside-RTH protection stays refused. [X]
- **Protective amendment boundaries:** Parts 1/3 accept method and no-suspension shadow; Part 2 values remain proposed/owner-applied. Separate entry/protective accounting prioritizes fast sell → backstop → TP rolls, retaining native gates/reserve. Proposed only: 5 groups×60/min×1.2=360 protective; 30 entries; 60 headroom=5 backstop+55 other/TP; 134 reads; native submit/modify 390/360; node/all calls 524/560; conditional later node 964. Current stricter caps bind. Exact-version paper-B e2e, exhaustive preregistered cohort/fixed `T_end`, all family trials, capacity, C.5 and code review remain required. Re-entry/rotation need a separate live_nonrenewing promotion after paper proof; the paper 600 s cooldown is uncalibrated and not adopted for live. [X]
- **Host residual:** old accepted placement is nslive/system services on wired workstation WSL, with **`os_isolation=false`**: administrators can reach keys/units. Bridge peer checks identify the bridge, not original caller. The only waived prerequisite is dead-man notification at Stage 1 (owner 2026-10-30); independent halt/reconciliation/reserve and all other gates stand. No host installation or cutover is certified. [L, X]
