# The pre-arm noise surface on minute bars (#141)

**Result: no cell of ADR-0019's documented grid comes near holding.** Across all 27 registered cells, our exit engine returned **−36.14 to −51.62 bps per trade** at 3 bps per side (−46.24 to −59.45 at 6 bps). On the same 1,025 entries, holding to 15:55 returned **+76.44/+70.42**. Every cell also loses to both C5 controls. Nothing is selected.

Pre-arm exits account for most of the gap in every cell: 94 to 124 bps of a 113 to 128 bps gap, at 3 bps. This is MODELED on #98's minute-bar proxy, at uncalibrated values. The range multiple cannot act on minute bars, so the 27 cells hold only 9 distinct results.

*Versioned (ruling (a), 2026-11-07): every number on this page is the committed engine's history. PR 2's engine run is closed in the ledger (attempt `pr2-a8282a4-r3`); see Checks.*

- **In every cell, pre-arm exits sell dips that recover.**
  - A wider buffer sells fewer of them: 591, 404 and 257 at giveback 0, for buffers 0.005, 0.01 and 0.02. Yet the gap narrows by at most 15.29 bps, from −127.87 to −112.58 at giveback 0.
  - The exits it still makes are deeper ones. At giveback 0, the engine's mean on its pre-arm exits goes from −96.39 to −215.47 bps, while holding those same entries returns +119.45 to +199.11.
- **A larger giveback arms more entries.**
  - Arming needs the bid above the armed lock, `max(entry + c_RT, H − max(floor, g·(H − entry)))`, and a larger g lowers that lock.
  - So pre-arm exits fall and gain-lock exits rise. At buffer 0.02 that is 257, 246 and 221 pre-arm, against 405, 442 and 512 gain-lock.
  - Each gain-lock exit also gives back more of the move, so the net effect is small and mostly negative: −36.14, −41.24 and −42.65 at buffer 0.02.
- **At 6 bps the proxy bid sits lower,** so the 0.005 buffer sells more (681 to 698 pre-arm exits). The ordering is the same: the widest buffer gives the best mean.
- **Take-profit:** 0 touches in every cell, as in #98. No held minute rose 10% above its prior high.
- **Chase:** off in every cell.

## Method
- **Registered first.** The grid is exactly trading ADR-0019's documented grids (northstar-trading@7c5f852a `docs/adr/0019-managed-exits-and-ranked-reentry.md`): buffer {0.005, 0.01, 0.02} (:251), range multiple {0.5, 1, 2} (:252) and giveback {0, 0.1, 0.25} (:140). Every other row stays at `ExitParams.starts()`. Its SHA-256 `8302f7c3…85c6` was posted on #141 before any run, and [registration.json](2026-11-06-exit-grid-141/registration.json) is that file, byte for byte.
- **Reserved before the run.** Each cell is one RUNNING, MODELED attempt, handed through the ledger inbox (#102) and appended at `ledger` 8f045fae as trials 112–138.
- **Run once, as registered.** `python -m sextant.research.exit_grid_141 run --attempt grid-1` ran at implementation `4656648` on a clean tree; the run refuses any other HEAD. It was one process, 22:02–22:53Z, under a 3 GB memory cap and a 2-hour timeout.
- **Outcome.** Its 27 COMPLETE rows went back through the inbox (rows SHA-256 `2190e309…82b7`) and are committed as [outcome.jsonl](2026-11-06-exit-grid-141/outcome.jsonl). [report.json](2026-11-06-exit-grid-141/report.json) holds every cell's metrics and attribution at both costs.
- **Tape, entries, costs and controls** are #98's, unchanged:
  - the pinned minute-bar proxy (northstar-trading@8256c16c `b_gain_protection.py:856–905`);
  - the recorded-entry anchor (:705–735) and the broker tick (:411–415);
  - 3 and 6 bps per side;
  - hold to 15:55, and C5-n22-k3-R-OFF on both fill models.
- **Chase off, R_w dropped** (#98 rulings). The range multiple scales R_w, so on minute bars it cannot act: in every (buffer, giveback) pair its three values give identical results, and a test checks that. The ledger counts 27 rows, but they hold only 9 distinct results.
- **Nothing is selected.** No cell becomes a start or a registry value (ADR-0018, ADR-0019).
  - The cohort spans three sessions: 9, 316 and 700 entries on October 26, 27 and 28. That is too few for ADR-0019's whole-session tests, and PBO is INCONCLUSIVE below `pbo_min_sessions`.
  - ADR-0019's adoption bar also needs a lower bound above hold-flat at stressed cost. Every cell's point estimate is at least 116 bps below hold at 6 bps.

## Not estimable on minute bars
- **Chase:** neither `chase_cap_fraction` nor `chase_min_interval_s` carries authority, so every fast sell rests at the bid proxy (#98).
- **R_w, and with it the range multiple:** the 10 s window holds no proxy trade. The tick grid is where this axis can act.
- **Persistence:** 3 quotes over 1 s becomes 3 consecutive minute lows, a slower test than the ADR's.
- **Arming:** only `bar_1m`. Its quote-run alternatives are sub-minute.
