## K3.5 Arms (frozen; all MODELED, rejects included)

Every arm runs on the §K3.3 names against K2 (arm K), with hold-flat
(arm H, no flag, no entry) retained because ADR-0019's adoption gate
needs it even though this detection study cannot satisfy that gate
(§K3.7). One full-cohort evaluation of one arm at one sealed
`params_hash` is one trial (§K3.10).

- **Arm (a) — ADR-0024 premarket gap rule (the OMH-type gap-open
  path).** Exactly ADR-0024's recorded rule, no more: the original
  fixed-range mode admits a name whose first-open gap is at least
  1%; trigger mode instead seals the first complete, aligned,
  bullish five-live-minute block meeting its explicit gap and
  `sum(close × volume)` thresholds; neither mode backfills a missed
  live range (ADR-0024 Decision, Premarket). Its recorded calibration
  values (`max(50000, 0.001 × ADV20)` and $1m prior-post turnover,
  retaining $5m candidate ADV and unknown-ADV exclusions) are a
  single-day paper calibration, not an optimum, and below-5% triggers
  stay an UNCALIBRATED candidate grid (ADR-0024). Source:
  Zarattini–Barbon–Aziz, SSRN 4729284, carried as an explicitly
  unvalidated premarket adaptation of an RTH reference (ADR-0024
  Evidence). This is the only arm that can in principle flag a
  first-print gap such as OMH (first print `04:00:00.055894` at
  +102%), which trailing-baseline arms structurally cannot
  (reconcile §5). The ruling schedules this arm first, for OMH, PARA
  and the six movers in the PR-3/PR-4 replays, under this freeze.
- **Arm (b) — mean-baseline RVOL against K2's median.** K2's normal is
  the per-session **median** of time-matched cumulative dollar volume
  (`src/sextant/ranking/baseline.py`). This arm replaces only the
  location statistic with the **mean** of the same prior sessions —
  the previous-days average of Zarattini–Barbon–Aziz §5 p.19, already
  the carried `mean_prior_opening_windows` denominator in
  `stocks_in_play.py` (its row is UNCALIBRATED, a disclosed
  interpretation of §3 p.14's notation) — and changes nothing else in
  the score. It is aimed at the measured coverage failure: 35 of 56
  premarket onsets (62.5%) had a zero baseline at onset in the
  screen, and that group's flags were hours late while the
  baseline-available group flagged before onset (reconcile §6). A
  zero mean stays zero: this arm does not invent volume, and any name
  whose baseline is still zero or unavailable is reported as such
  (§K3.6), never imputed. TA-Lib is recorded as the ruling names it,
  unpinned (§K3.1, §K3.12).
- **Arm (c) — ADR-0024 keep-alive on membership against K2 churn.**
  K2's measured failure is persistence, not only lateness: 132 of 159
  movers first entered the top 100 before their onset and then
  dropped out, leaving 12 of 159 in the top 100 at onset (reconcile
  §4). This arm applies ADR-0024's recorded keep-alive — it preserves
  eligible membership through incomplete session inputs; current
  entry qualification remains required, quotas win, and 20:00 resets
  range/attempt against the new close (ADR-0024 Decision, Mover
  arming) — to the K2 ranking state, and is judged on persistence at
  onset (§K3.2). It changes no score and no threshold. ADR-0024
  records keep-alive as selection/implementation evidence, not
  measured alpha; this arm supplies the missing paired measurement,
  nothing more.
- **Arm (d) — Page's CUSUM as a research-only volume mechanism.**
  Page's one-sided CUSUM, `S_t = max(0, S_{t-1} + x_t − k)`, alarm
  when `S_t > h` (Page 1954), on a sealed dimensionless dollar-volume

## 2. The score under test (frozen)

The unit under test is exactly the carried K2 implementation at the sealed
implementation SHA: `src/sextant/ranking/engine.py` (`advance_mover`,
`rank_movers`), `baseline.py` (`BaselineTable.cumulative`) and
`registry.py` (`Registry`, `ROWS`), themselves carries of
northstar-lanes@`00203275944a22655b3e14b221cfde9a265d549a` `mover.py`,
`baseline.py`, `trade_window.py`, `registry.py` and `contracts.py` (see the
ranking note and manifest row "Fixture ranking, generations and freshness
refusal"). If the sealed code differs from the formulas below, the code at
the sealed SHA governs and the discrepancy stops the run; the formulas are
written here so that check is possible. All money arithmetic is `Decimal`;
no float enters the score.

For one symbol in one feed (`sip` daytime, `boats` overnight, never pooled)
and one segment (PRE, RTH, POST, OVERNIGHT; §3), re-evaluated on every
eligible trade:

- `move = last_price / prior_close − 1`, where `last_price` is the most
  recent price-eligible trade price at or before the current trade and
  `prior_close` is the most recently completed official regular-session
  close, split-adjusted, on the same adjustment basis as `last_price`:
  POST uses that day's close, OVERNIGHT the session just ended, PRE/RTH
  the previous session (ADR-0003 K2). An undocumented reference gives
  `None`; there is no fallback close. The RTH open-based move is recorded
  separately and never enters the score.
- Segment dollar volume is the sum of `price × size` over volume-eligible
  trades from the segment start. It is defined only with a covered prefix:
  a late attachment without a verified prefix leaves the ratio unknown for
  the whole segment (`segment_prefix_unknown`). The actual closing print
  belongs to no extended segment, and closing-cross conditions update
  neither price nor volume history, exactly as the engine refuses them.
- The baseline normal at elapsed offset `s` seconds into the segment is the
  median, across prior sessions only, of each prior session's own
  cumulative dollar-volume curve read at the same offset (a median of
  per-session values, never a full-session total and never a difference of
  medians for cumulative queries). Within-minute bar curves are linearly
  interpolated and labelled as that approximation; offsets outside a
  curve's coverage contribute nothing (no zero, no extrapolation). A
  baseline containing the current or a future session is refused.
- `dvol_ratio = segment_dollar_volume / normal_dollar_volume`, defined
  only when the prefix is covered and the normal exists and is > 0.
- `trades_accel = recent_trades × baseline_seconds / (prior_trades ×
  recent_seconds)` over the registry windows below, defined only when the
  prior window is covered and non-zero.
- `score = move × dvol_ratio`. Rank is over **all computable rows**
  (score and acceleration both known), descending score, then descending
  `trades_accel`, then symbol ascending: a strict total order, rank 1
  first. Dollar volume, share volume, price, trade rate and spread never
  form the score and no price or ADV floor gates ranking (ADR-0003 K1/K2).

The four K2 gates — `move ≥ mover_min_move`, `move_1m > 0`,
`trades_accel ≥ mover_min_trades_accel`, `dvol_ratio ≥
mover_min_dvol_ratio` — are entry-qualification flags, not ranking floors:
excluded entries still rank with flags (ADR-0003 K2). The primary endpoints
in §5 therefore rank all computable rows. A secondary, predeclared table
repeats recall restricted to gate-qualified rows; it is descriptive only
and cannot replace the primary table after results are seen.

Every tunable is one of the twelve registry rows frozen by name and unit in
`registry.py` `ROWS` (`mover_min_move`, `mover_min_trades_accel`,
`mover_min_dvol_ratio`, `move_1m_window_s`, `move_5m_window_s`,
`trades_accel_window_s`, `trades_accel_baseline_min`,
`publish_min_interval_s`, `generation_max_age_s`, `heartbeat_max_age_s`,
`input_max_age_s`, `top_n`). This protocol freezes no numeric value for
them: values, sources, calibration states and last-change dates arrive as

(ADR-0018 L3) and are bound at the seal by `params_hash`. The values in

row still ranks with flags, and the flag travels into every table that
uses the row.

## 4. Truth and onset: hindsight labels, never features (frozen)

Truth and onset are computed from the session's own recorded price path
after the fact, for labelling only. No truth or onset quantity may enter
any feature, rank, gate or baseline. Both are defined from price versus
the official prior close alone, so neither can be circular with the K2
score, its gates, or its ranks.

- **Session move path.** For a symbol-session (one PIT identity on one
  trading date, overnight assigned as in §3), let the move at each
  price-eligible trade be `price / prior_close − 1` on the §2 basis.
  `truth_move` is the maximum of that path over the trading date, with
  the feed and segment of the maximizing trade retained. Feeds are never
  mixed inside one ranking generation (§2), but the hindsight maximum
  may be attained in any segment; its segment and feed labels say where.
- **Truth mover (primary).** A symbol-session with `truth_move ≥ 0.05`
  (Decimal, inclusive). The 5% line is the pinned missed-movers
  witness's mover threshold (its table's +5% column and "11 observed
  +5% movers", carried in the extreme-mover evidence note), adopted
  here as an UNCALIBRATED evaluation label — it is not an entry
  threshold, not the registry's `mover_min_move`, and not evidence that
  5% is economically meaningful. The +10% crossing is recorded
  descriptively in the same witness's shape; it creates no second truth
  set and no additional endpoint family.
- **Onset (primary anchor).** For a truth mover, onset is the first
  price-eligible trade, in event-time order within its feed and
  segment, at which the causal move first reaches `≥ 0.05`. Its
  evaluation time `τ` is that trade's known-at time as recorded
  (`max(ts_event, ts_init)` on the trade, consistent with the engine's
  `known_at`); for bar-fidelity sessions the onset is known only at bar
  close, and `τ` is the bar's close clock, labelled as such (the
  witness's "minute starts, known only at minute end" caveat, frozen).
  Onset is therefore the hindsight moment the move had provably begun
  in size, against which "ranked as it starts" can be judged without
  consulting any detector.
- **What onset is not.** This is not the K3 surge/onset detector
  (anomalous short-window dollar volume, minimum prints, move off the
  quiet price, rising at the decision, first leg within
  `first_leg_window_s`; ADR-0003 K3, ADR-0024). That detector's
  experiment is NOT_RUN and its upstream onset convergence is
  REDESIGN-NEED (candidate-history audit); this protocol builds no
  detector, fits no quiet price, and evaluates no K3 threshold. Event
  time, first moved print, detector flag and known-at remain distinct
  clocks (ADR-0024); only the price-path anchor above is frozen here.
  A future detector evaluation needs its own sourced protocol.
- **Recorded per mover** (the witness table's shape, its cohort not
  reused): PIT identity and symbol, prior close with source, first
  trade time, +5% onset time and `τ`, +10% time or its absence,
  `truth_move` with its segment/feed, first top-30 and first top-100
  entry times (§5), rank at onset, and every unknown reason. A trading
  date with no truth mover has undefined recall (§5): it is counted and
  reported as a no-truth session, never scored 0 and never dropped.

## 5. Generations and recall@30/100 per session (frozen)

**What is ranked, when.** Scores recompute on every eligible trade (§2).
The evaluation reads ranking **generations** — what a consumer could
actually know — published at most once per `publish_min_interval_s`,
each carrying identity, coverage and known-at time. A stale, invalid or
incomplete generation is refused with no fallback to an earlier good one
(ADR-0003 K6); the separately tagged top-N projection is incomplete for
consumption and is never scored in place of the full generation
(ranking note). The rank current at time `t` in a feed/segment is the
rank in the latest valid full generation of that feed/segment with
known-at `≤ t` (inclusive). If no such generation exists, the symbol is
unranked at `t`. Ranks from different feeds or segments are never merged
into one list.

**Endpoints.** For `k ∈ {30, 100}` — the house recall depths already in
use (the saved-scanner witness reports @30 and @100; the design records
6/41 and 25/41 at its earliest publication under its own, different
truth, which this protocol does not reuse):

- `hit@k(m)`: truth mover `m` has rank `≤ k` (inclusive) in the
  generation current at its onset time `τ(m)`, in its onset feed and
  segment. A mover unranked at `τ` — including score-unknown for any
  recorded reason — is a miss, with the reason retained.
- **Primary:** per trading date `d` with truth set `T(d)`,
  `recall@k(d) = |{m ∈ T(d) : hit@k(m)}| / |T(d)|`, reported as the
  count pair (hits, `|T(d)|`) beside the ratio. Undefined when
  `|T(d)| = 0` (§4). This onset-anchored, per-session pair at both
  depths is the evaluation's primary result.
- **Timing (predeclared, same trial):** per mover and depth, the first
  generation time at or after its onset-segment start, in its onset
  feed and segment, at which it enters the
  top `k`, and the signed lead `τ(m) − first-entry` (positive: ranked
  before onset size was reached; negative: late; absent: never entered,
  a censored miss, never an infinite or imputed lead). Reported as
  counts and quantiles over observed leads with the censored count
  beside them, per segment and overall.
- **Trajectory (predeclared, descriptive):** per trading date and
  depth, recall recomputed with every mover judged in the generation
  current at each segment's close (that segment's last valid generation
  at or before segment end). This shows when recall arrives; it cannot
  substitute for the onset-anchored primary after results are seen.
- Top `k` when fewer than `k` rows are computable is all computable
  rows; the computable-universe size is reported with every recall so a
  shallow universe cannot flatter the ratio invisibly.

**Aggregation and dependence.** Per-session results come first (the W2
row's "per session"); pooled micro-average (summed hits over summed
truth) and session macro-average (mean of per-session ratios over
sessions with defined recall) are both reported, neither hidden, with
session counts. Symbol-sessions inside one trading date share a market
day and are not independent replications; the trading date is the block
(ADR-0019's whole-session block principle), and segment, feed,
price-band and year breakdowns are descriptive strata, not independent
looks. This protocol freezes counts and ratios as its confirmatory
output and claims no interval: no sourced interval method for recall
proportions exists in the repo, so any interval must be sourced and
named at the seal or reported UNAVAILABLE — never attached post hoc.
Walk-forward, CPCV, DSR and PBO are **not applicable** to this
single-arm descriptive recall measurement (there is no fitted choice,
no return series and no selection to deflate) and are recorded as such;
they bind in full, with this trial inside the cumulative count, the
moment any later protocol selects among scores, thresholds or arms
using this cohort (§7). Costs, fills and P&L play no part: recall is a
pre-economic ranking measurement, and no cost model is frozen because
none is consumed; any economic claim needs a separate protocol with
ADR-0019's costed, paired machinery.

## 6. Matched non-mover controls (frozen)

Controls answer one question: at the moments movers reached onset, how
much of the top 30/100 would comparable non-movers have occupied
anyway? Without that base rate, recall alone cannot distinguish ranking
signal from a hot tape.

- **Control pool.** Same trading date, same PIT universe (§3),
  symbol-sessions that are not truth movers (`truth_move < 0.05`) and
  that appear as a row (computable or score-unknown, with reasons) in
  the generation judging their matched mover. No-trade symbol-sessions
  have no row and enter neither numerator nor denominator; they are
  counted separately so the exclusion stays visible. Seed losers, seed
  absence and scanner-archive membership never define controls
  (ADR-0027, §3).
- **Matching (exact-cell, knowable-only).** Each truth mover is matched
  on variables knowable at its onset `τ`: trading date (exact), onset
  segment (exact), onset feed (exact), and ADR-0003 K7 price band of
  the prior close (≥ $5, $1–$5, < $1; boundaries: $5 belongs to the top
  band, $1 to the middle band, exactly as the labels read). The
  mover's control set is **every** eligible non-mover in its cell —
  full-cell controls, no sampling, no seed, no ratio to tune. A mover
  whose cell contains no eligible non-mover is reported unmatched; its
  cell is never widened after results are seen. Labels stratify and
  never gate ranking itself (ADR-0003 K7/K8); cap bucket stays unused
  while point-in-time shares are null.
- **Control endpoint.** In the same generation that judges the mover's
  `hit@k` (§5), the control occupancy rate is the fraction of its
  matched controls with rank `≤ k`; unranked controls count as not in
  the top `k` with reasons retained. The predeclared contrast, per
  trading date and depth, is `recall@k(d)` beside the mean matched
  control occupancy over that date's matched movers, with mover,
  matched and unmatched counts. It is descriptive: no significance
  claim is frozen (§5), and a favourable contrast is not an adoption
  gate.
- **Named limit.** No sourced distance, propensity or optimal-matching
  method exists in this repo, and the candidate-history audit records
  the frozen ordinary-control protocol as a blocker precisely because
  Rule 1 stops unsupported control construction. This protocol
  therefore freezes only exact stratification on sourced labels,
  claims no causal matching and no covariate balance beyond its four
  exact factors, and leaves richer matching to a future protocol that
  brings its own pinned method. If even these labels cannot be
  supplied point-in-time for a session, that session reports recall
  with controls UNAVAILABLE rather than controls improvised.

## 7. Trial count bound to the one TrialLog (frozen)

There is one cumulative `TrialLog` across MODELED, SIMULATED and
BROKER-PAPER; tiers and block sets are never pooled and history is never
reset (ADR-0018 L9, ADR-0019). This evaluation touches it exactly as
follows, and in no other way:

- **One arm, one tier.** A single arm — K2 exactly as in §2 at one
  sealed implementation SHA and one sealed `params_hash` over one
  sealed cohort — at tier **MODELED** (historical replay over recorded
  data; no live feed, no broker). One full-cohort evaluation attempt is
  **one trial**: one arm-run registered before any cohort outcome is
  read, following the retained writer contract in
  `src/sextant/validation/trials.py` (13-column schema; identity checks;
  no delete or reset API; reruns require fresh arm-run IDs). The
  reservation requested from the ledger writer is therefore **1 trial**
  for the sealed attempt, plus the standing rule that every failed,
  pruned, repeated or re-sealed attempt is an additional counted trial
  — never a replacement that erases its predecessor.
- **No search inside this trial.** No variant, ablation, threshold
  sweep or registry-value comparison is run, reported or selected
  under this protocol. ADR-0003's experiment family (MOVER-v2,
  CF-EVENT-LEX-v1, CapFocus-Borda-v1, frozen CURRENT-native, hold-flat
  and CASH comparators, ratio/acceleration/surge/accumulation
  ablations) is separate work needing its own preregistration; nothing
  here pre-registers, implies or spends trials on it. The two depths,
  the timing and trajectory tables, the gate-qualified secondary table
  and the +10% descriptive column are predeclared views of the single
  arm's single run, frozen in §§4–6; choosing a favourite among them
  after results would be a new selection and a new protocol.
- **Identity binding.** The trial record must bind, through the
  writer's required identity fields (`arm_id`, `arm_run_id`,
  `implementation_sha`, `factor_mask`, sandbox config and its hash,
  evidence weights and their hash, `params_hash`, `tier`,
  `block_set_id`, funding-uncertainty counts, condition returns):
  this protocol's frozen SHA-256, the W1 manifest hash, per-session
  data hashes or their sealed index hash, the cohort session list as
  the block set (whole trading dates, §5), the ranking implementation
  SHA, the evaluation harness SHA, and the registry `params_hash`.
  Params and config carry Decimal strings only, never floats, as the
  writer enforces. Funding counts are explicitly null (unknown), not
  zero: no funding quantity is measured here. Code binding remains
  whatever the writer records (currently UNVERIFIED in the retained
  slice) and is quoted as recorded, never upgraded by this protocol.
- **Recall has no returns — encoding to be confirmed, not invented.**
  The retained schema's return-shaped fields (`daily_net_returns`,
  `fold_scores`, `objective`) have no honest values for a recall
  measurement, and native returns must not be invented to fill them
  (ledger-merit note). This protocol proposes registering the arm-run
  with empty `days`, return and fold arrays and a null objective — the
  writer requires return arrays aligned to `days`, so sessions cannot
  be listed without returns — binding the cohort instead through
  `block_set_id` and the sealed session-list hash in config, with
  recall results living in the §10 report bound by
  hash — **subject to the ledger writer's confirmation** in the
  reservation request that this encoding is accepted under the
  retained contract. If the writer refuses it, compute stays NOT_RUN;
  no side ledger, parallel JSONL outcome writer or schema fork may be
  created to route around the refusal.
- **Cumulative count.** The cumulative N and any effective-N used by
  any later inference are the writer's inference-time snapshots,
  frozen when taken (ADR-0019); `effective_n` stays null while
  inference remains deferred, and no ONC or correlation claim is made
  here. This protocol asserts no current cumulative number: canonical
  custody (protected ref/path, #110) and the inference layer (#147)
  are recorded as unresolved in the ledger-merit note, no ledger file
  exists in this checkout, and an audit's zero new runs is never the
  historical search count (handbook lesson). The reservation request
  asks the writer to state the canonical location and the append-time
  `n_trials` this trial will carry; until it answers, the count bound
  to this protocol is "1 + attempts, at an UNKNOWN cumulative offset".
- **One look.** Cohort outcomes are read once, after the seal (§9)
  and after registration. Any further read that informs a choice —
  re-sealing with different registry values, redefining truth,
  onset, cells or depths, subsetting sessions — is a new trial under a
  new protocol version (§11), and the revealed cohort becomes
  development data for confirmatory purposes (ADR-0019). Engineering
  reruns that read no outcome (crashes before results, byte-identical
  reproduction checks) still count as attempts when they register
  arm-runs; the writer's record, not intent, decides.

## 8. Decision rule (frozen: measurement only)

The sealed run produces the §10 tables and stops. No value of recall,
lead or control contrast adopts, calibrates, promotes or retires
anything: K2 remains the incumbent house score, UNCALIBRATED, exactly
as before the run, whatever the tables say. A negative or embarrassing
result is recorded and kept and is never re-run until it turns positive
(research lane rule). Any proposal that follows — a calibrated
threshold, a variant comparison, an entry claim, a sim or paper step —
starts from its own preregistration citing this result, carries this
trial in its cumulative count, and meets ADR-0019's paired,
stressed-cost adoption machinery there, not here. Paper operation is
neither gated nor licensed by this measurement.
