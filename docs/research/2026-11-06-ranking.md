## N1.5 Arms and the shared entry/protection chain (frozen)

Every trading arm is one §K3.5 flag source driving one identical
chain. The chain, frozen once here:

1. **Arming.** ADR-0004 N1 arms the first leg "inside
   `first_leg_window_s` of a surge or catalyst plus surge". The one
   measured substitution of this protocol is the arming event: the
   arm's **first** K3 flag on that name (§N1.6), at its known-at
   clock. One arming per name per arm; later flags, re-entry and
   rotation-after-entry belong to ADR-0006's own later measurement
   and are out of scope. A name an arm never flags has no arming and
   writes its refusal row (§N1.10).
2. **First-leg pick.** #83's reviewed pick on the SIP tape
   (`b_e2e_cohort.py` `find_run` with `reviewed_rule`): scans for the
   first run of `first_leg_trades_count` non-decreasing
   price-eligible prints above the quiet price (the print before
   the run's first print), spanning at least `first_leg_span_s`,
   last print at most `first_leg_max_move` above quiet; a lower
   print restarts the run and a print at or below quiet empties it.
   Frozen restriction for this protocol: only runs whose first
   print is at or after the arming clock and which complete inside
   `first_leg_window_s` of it are eligible — the window is N1's own,
   and a run completed before the flag was knowable is look-ahead,
   never an entry.
3. **Fresh-ask gate.** At run completion, the entry price is the
   then-current ask: the last quote at or before the confirming
   print, at most `max_data_age_s` old, two-sided (bid < ask), with
   `max(run last, ask)` inside the same `first_leg_max_move` cap
   (`b_e2e_cohort.py` `_entry_at`; the same freshness gate as
   `src/sextant/entry/replay.py`). Any failure is a refusal row —
   thin quote, stale quote, crossed quote or cap breach — never a
   loosened fill.
4. **Execution and protection.** The pick is replayed in the
   Nautilus `BacktestEngine` in #83's shape (`b_e2e.py`): a LIMIT
   buy at the sealed entry price for the seal-bound fixed quantity
   (§N1.8), and on position opened the reduce-only
   `trailing_stop_market` at 250 basis points,
   `TrailingOffsetType.BASIS_POINTS`, `TriggerType.LAST_PRICE` —
   B's recorded 2.5% level. The engine's exit fill at the
   triggering print is the protected exit. Wiring the cohort pick
   into the engine is build work on the rebased Part 1 branch; the
   seal requires its parity witness first (§N1.12): on #83's
   committed six-mover fixture, the engine chain must reproduce the
   cohort walk's entry and exit per case before any number under
   this protocol exists.
5. **Hold-flat.** The identical entry fill with no protection
   submitted, exited at the §N1.4 window-end mark, charged §N1.7
   costs identically. Hold-flat is computed for every entered name,
   including HELD names (whose mark is the same print), so Table
   B's pairing never selects on the trail having tripped.
6. **Refusals are results.** No flag, no run in window, cap breach,
   thin/stale/crossed quote, no slot (secondary read only), missing
   tape/quote/close and HELD each write their MODELED row with the
   reason (§N1.10). The OMH-type case is expected here, not trimmed:
   a first print far beyond the 3% cap refuses, and that refusal is
   arm (a)'s measured answer for gap-opens.

## N1.7 Costs (frozen: identities and levels; values at the seal)

Returns are net at two frozen levels — **1× and 2× all costs**
(16:18 ruling; ADR-0019: preserve the sign at 2×) — with a
predeclared **3× slippage-only inspection** (ADR-0019) in the same
family. The components, each stated per ADR-0019's hygiene line:

- **Spread.** Entry fills at the ask, so the entry half-spread is
  already inside the fill price and is never deducted twice
  (ADR-0019: fees already in net P&L are not deducted twice). The
  quoted half-spreads at entry and at exit are recorded per name
  from the sealed quotes as descriptive receipts.
- **Regulatory fees.** Per share, ADR-0005's recorded formula
  `c_RT = entry × sec31_rate + min(qty × taf_per_share, taf_cap) ÷
  qty + one tick` (`src/sextant/exits/policy.py`), charged once per
  round trip. The formula is frozen; the rates are not in this
  text — they arrive as reviewed registry rows carrying their dated
  SEC/FINRA notices, bound at the seal (§N1.12). No rate in a
  fixture or witness file is authority.
- **Market impact.** ADR-0004 N7's recorded charging — spread and
  square-root impact at 1× and 2× — as a per-side deduction
  `coefficient × price × sqrt(quantity ÷ volume_basis)`, Decimal
  throughout. The square-root form is the ADR's named model; the
  coefficient and the volume basis (eligible volume over a sealed
  lookback ending at the side's own clock) are seal-bound rows with
  their sources. No sourced coefficient exists in this repo at this
  freeze (searched `src/`, `lanes/`, `docs/adr/`); until the seal
  binds one, impact is UNAVAILABLE and the costed tables stay
  NOT_RUN — impact is never defaulted to zero and called costed.
- **Broker commission.** No sourced schedule exists in this repo
  (searched as above; ADR-0004 records only that "the fee bound is
  declared, not checked against its model"). If the seal binds no
  sourced schedule, every table carries the label "regulatory fees
  and impact only; broker commission UNAVAILABLE" — a missing
  component stays visible, never a silent zero.
- **Borrow.** None: long only (ADR-0004 N3), flat inside the sealed
  intraday window; no borrow quantity exists to state.
- **Levels.** 2× doubles `c_RT` and both impact sides; the 3×
  inspection triples impact only. #83's zero-rate `MakerTakerFeeModel`
  settings are fixture settings, not authority: the engine runs
  gross and §N1.7's deductions are applied in the harness's return
  accounting, in Decimal, identically to protected and hold-flat.
  A gross (zero-cost) column may be printed only labelled GROSS
  beside the net tables, never as the 1× table.

## N1.9 Dependence, replication, multiplicity; metrics and tests

- **Dependence:** symbol-sessions inside one trading date share a
  market day; the trading date is the block (ADR-0019's
  whole-session block principle, circular blocks retaining the
  symbol cross-section). Per-session tables come first. Segment,
  truth/control and baseline-availability strata are descriptive,
  not independent looks.
- **Replication:** one session (2026-11-09) yields a development
  table only. Any stability or merit reading needs ADR-0019's
  floors — at least 30 independent flat-to-flat round trips over at
  least 5 sessions covering every traded session type, at measured
  MinTRL with PSR ≥ 0.95 — with walk-forward/CPCV discipline
  (purge and embargo each at least the label/holding horizon) and
  an untouched one-shot 12–24-month holdout at the end. HELD and
  refused names are not round trips. Negative tables are recorded
  and kept, never re-run until positive.
- **Multiplicity:** one confirmatory look per sealed run. The
  family is every arm × cost level (1×, 2×; the 3× inspection
  inside it) read together. Intervals and tests are ADR-0019's
  named machinery only: Romano–Wolf StepM across the family on
  stacked arm×condition columns (`arch` 8.0.0, `bootstrap="cbb"`,
  α = 1 − `adoption_lower_bound_level`, every column must reject),
  studentized by block-bootstrap mean SE with the block length
  calibrated on training data (Ledoit–Wolf Algorithm 3.1) and a
  longer-block sensitivity; further preregistered looks use
  Bonferroni. `adoption_lower_bound_level` and `pbo_min_sessions`
  have no registry rows on gate at this freeze (searched): until
  the seal binds reviewed rows, intervals are UNAVAILABLE and the
  tables stand as paired point measurements with their counts —
  never dressed with an invented level. DSR > 0.95 (effective trial
  count, §N1.10), PBO ≤ 0.05 (CSCV, S=16; INCONCLUSIVE below
  `pbo_min_sessions`) and PSR ≥ 0.95 bind only at an adoption
  reading (§N1.11), which one session cannot reach. No best-arm
  selection after looking: choosing a favourite arm, cost level,
  sensitivity or subset after results is a new selection and a new
  protocol version. Outcomes are read once, after the seal and
  after trial registration (§N1.10).
- **Metrics (frozen list):** per arm, session and stratum —
  planned/flagged/armed/entered counts; refusal counts by class
  (§N1.5 step 6); Table A clocks and summaries (§N1.2); entered
  share at/before `τ`; Table B net protected and hold-flat returns
  at 1×/2× with both paired differences; HELD count with marks;

  name (entry, high, level, exit — #83's report shape); quoted
  half-spreads at entry/exit; and the per-name unknown reasons.
  Empty denominators render as undefined-with-count (K2 §5), never
  as zero.

## K3.2 Estimand and pairing (frozen)

- **Question:** does each arm in §K3.5, paired name-by-name against K2,
  reach the early stage at or before onset more often and more
  selectively than K2 does?
- **Primary estimand, per arm:** over the sealed truth movers
  (§K3.3), the paired per-name quantities (i) first-flag latency
  (first arm flag − onset `τ`, §K3.6; negative is early; absent is a
  censored miss, never imputed), summarized as p50/p90 over detected
  names with the censored count beside them; (ii) recall at onset
  (share whose first flag is at or before `τ`); and (iii) false flags
  per symbol-hour on the sealed controls. For arm (c), whose output is
  membership rather than a flag, (i)–(ii) read on top-100 membership
  at `τ` given first entry before `τ` (persistence), with first-entry
  lead as in the K2 protocol below (§5 timing).
- **Secondary (predeclared, same trials):** first flag inside
  ±60/±120/±300 s of onset; per-name flag counts; K2 rank at onset;
  baseline availability at onset per arm (the zero-baseline share,
  §K3.5 (b)); never-crossed and all-control false rates reported
  separately.
- **Pairing:** every arm and K2 are read on **the same names, the same
  sessions, the same `τ`, the same control list and the same
  symbol-hour denominator**. A name missing a required input for one
  arm is reported unavailable for that arm with its reason; paired
  contrasts use the names available to both sides, with the
  planned/observed/paired/excluded counts reconciled (§K3.12). Cohorts
  built under different close definitions are never pooled (§K3.3).

## K3.3 Universe, cohorts and controls (frozen)

- **Truth movers (primary):** symbol-sessions whose price path reaches
  `≥ +5%` against the official prior close, under exactly the truth
  and onset-anchor definitions frozen in the K2 protocol below (§4:
  price-path labels only, never features). This protocol does not
  redefine truth; it reuses that frozen label so K2 and every K3 arm
  judge the same events. The first sealed cohort is the 159-mover
  2026-11-09 set under the bar-close definition, plus OMH recorded
  explicitly (it is already inside that set's session; it is named
  because it is the binding gap-open counterexample, §K3.5 (a)).
- **Close definition (frozen choice, one sensitivity):** the cohort
  close is the **last bar close** (the 159 definition). The tape-last
  alternative, which changes 7 names' membership (155 common), is a
  predeclared sensitivity reported beside the primary table, never
  pooled with it (reconcile §3 note 1; UNKNOWN U3 settles only by this
  freeze, not by either leg's preference).
- **Controls (one shared list):** one frozen control list and one
  symbol-hour denominator for every arm: the sealed session candidates
  with move `< +5%` and premarket dollar volume `≥ $1m`, taken whole or
  by the deterministic sample bound at the seal. Never-crossed and
  all-control false rates are reported separately. The two screen
  legs' control sets (5 of 40 shared) are not reused: their false
  rates are not comparable and neither list survives as a default
  (reconcile §3 note 2; U4).
- **Sessions:** the sealed session list is bound at the seal
  (§K3.12). The 2026-11-09 session alone supports only the
  development-table reading in the freeze record; any stability or
  adoption-directed claim needs the replication floor in §K3.9.
  Universe membership, survivor and point-in-time labelling follow
  the K2 protocol below (§3) unchanged; a result inherits its
  `survivor_only` / `pit_verified` labels verbatim.

## K3.4 Data, PIT and windows (frozen)

- **Tape:** SIP trades at trade-tick fidelity, all conditions
  unfiltered in the primary run, full session window `04:00–11:35`
  ET for the 2026-11-09 cohort (the window both screen legs'
  session-first onset needs; per-mover truncated windows are refused
  because they miss earlier transient prints, reconcile §3 note 3).
  For later sealed sessions the window is the full PRE+RTH span bound
  at the seal; no window is chosen after seeing a name's path.
- **Baselines:** prior completed sessions only (for 2026-11-09:
  2026-11-02..06), time-matched per segment; a baseline containing
  the current or a future session is refused. Features must be
  knowable by decision time and bars closed (ADR-0019). Event time,
  first moved print, flag time and known-at stay distinct clocks
  (ADR-0024); replay stamps are not operational receipt latency (U7).
- **Predeclared sensitivities (same trials, reported whole):**
  condition-filtered tape (U9); tick-level prior-session baseline
  against the minute-bar baseline (U10); sustained-onset anchors

## K3.5 Arms (frozen; all MODELED, rejects included)

Every arm runs on the §K3.3 names against K2 (arm K), with hold-flat
(arm H, no flag, no entry) retained because ADR-0019's adoption gate
needs it even though this detection study cannot satisfy that gate
(§K3.7). One full-cohort evaluation of one arm at one sealed
`params_hash` is one trial (§K3.10).

- **Arm (a) — ADR-0024 premarket gap rule (the OMH-type gap-open
  path).** Exactly ADR-0024's recorded rule, no more: the original
  fixed-range mode admits a name whose first-open gap is at least
  1%; trigger mode instead seals the first complete, aligned,
  bullish five-live-minute block meeting its explicit gap and
  `sum(close × volume)` thresholds; neither mode backfills a missed
  live range (ADR-0024 Decision, Premarket). Its recorded calibration
  values (`max(50000, 0.001 × ADV20)` and $1m prior-post turnover,
  retaining $5m candidate ADV and unknown-ADV exclusions) are a
  single-day paper calibration, not an optimum, and below-5% triggers
  stay an UNCALIBRATED candidate grid (ADR-0024). Source:
  Zarattini–Barbon–Aziz, SSRN 4729284, carried as an explicitly
  unvalidated premarket adaptation of an RTH reference (ADR-0024
  Evidence). This is the only arm that can in principle flag a
  first-print gap such as OMH (first print `04:00:00.055894` at
  +102%), which trailing-baseline arms structurally cannot
  (reconcile §5). The ruling schedules this arm first, for OMH, PARA
  and the six movers in the PR-3/PR-4 replays, under this freeze.
- **Arm (b) — mean-baseline RVOL against K2's median.** K2's normal is
  the per-session **median** of time-matched cumulative dollar volume
  (`src/sextant/ranking/baseline.py`). This arm replaces only the
  location statistic with the **mean** of the same prior sessions —
  the previous-days average of Zarattini–Barbon–Aziz §5 p.19, already
  the carried `mean_prior_opening_windows` denominator in
  `stocks_in_play.py` (its row is UNCALIBRATED, a disclosed
  interpretation of §3 p.14's notation) — and changes nothing else in
  the score. It is aimed at the measured coverage failure: 35 of 56
  premarket onsets (62.5%) had a zero baseline at onset in the
  screen, and that group's flags were hours late while the
  baseline-available group flagged before onset (reconcile §6). A
  zero mean stays zero: this arm does not invent volume, and any name
  whose baseline is still zero or unavailable is reported as such
  (§K3.6), never imputed. TA-Lib is recorded as the ruling names it,
  unpinned (§K3.1, §K3.12).
- **Arm (c) — ADR-0024 keep-alive on membership against K2 churn.**
  K2's measured failure is persistence, not only lateness: 132 of 159
  movers first entered the top 100 before their onset and then
  dropped out, leaving 12 of 159 in the top 100 at onset (reconcile
  §4). This arm applies ADR-0024's recorded keep-alive — it preserves
  eligible membership through incomplete session inputs; current
  entry qualification remains required, quotas win, and 20:00 resets
  range/attempt against the new close (ADR-0024 Decision, Mover
  arming) — to the K2 ranking state, and is judged on persistence at
  onset (§K3.2). It changes no score and no threshold. ADR-0024
  records keep-alive as selection/implementation evidence, not
  measured alpha; this arm supplies the missing paired measurement,
  nothing more.
- **Arm (d) — Page's CUSUM as a research-only volume mechanism.**
  Page's one-sided CUSUM, `S_t = max(0, S_{t-1} + x_t − k)`, alarm
  when `S_t > h` (Page 1954), on a sealed dimensionless dollar-volume
  ratio series, carried by `PageHinkleyArm`
  (`src/sextant/research/detectors/page_hinkley.py`, river 0.26.1 at
  the manifest pin). The reconcile adopts Page as the cited
  volume-anomaly **mechanism** for a preregistered follow-on — not an
  onset clock: screen flags were minutes early and frequent, not
  first-second or selective (reconcile §2.1). River's constructor
  defaults are **rejected as market parameters** (the adapter ships
  every parameter `UNAVAILABLE`); this arm's `k`/`h` equivalents,
  gates and refractory are pre-declared registry rows bound at the
  seal (§K3.8), never inherited defaults.
- **Arm K — K2 comparator.** K2 exactly as frozen in the protocol
  below (§2), read as generations (§5 there): rank at onset, first
  top-30/top-100 entry and lead, top-100 membership at onset.
- **Arm H — hold-flat.** No flag; the zero against which selectivity
  and any later return-based gate are read.

**Rejected arms, recorded not deleted** (reconcile §§1–2; each counts
in §K3.10): Kleinberg burst detection (non-causal batch fit);
Adams–MacKay BOCPD as an alarm (no posterior-to-alarm rule exists;
inventing one is self-invention); Hawkes intensity (no cited causal
alarm); river ADWIN (no direction; stays a frozen follow-on);
KSWIN (window latency, no direction); ruptures (offline
segmentation); river binary DDM/EDDM (wrong input); the
non-zero-median, at-least-3-sessions thin-baseline rule as a *cited*
rule (no source found; both screen legs' paired variants showed no
gain — it may appear only as a labelled house diagnostic, never as
an arm claiming a source); and river's constructor defaults as
parameters (above). The **K3 composite** — Page's statistic plus
ADR-0003's minimum-prints, quiet-price-move and rising clauses —
stays **HOLD**: no cited detector supplies those clauses end to end,
and no result under this protocol promotes the composite (§K3.11).

## K3.6 Onset and flags (frozen)

- **Onset `τ` (primary):** the session-first price-eligible trade at
  or above prior close × 1.05, on the K2 protocol's §4 basis, read
  over the full §K3.4 window. **Predeclared sensitivity:** sustained
  anchors (the move held for 5 s and for 60 s) reported beside the
  primary; the choice between transient and sustained onset is never
  made after results (U2).
- **Flag:** an arm's first alarm after its warm-up **and** after its
  baseline is available. Unavailable seconds are **missing, not
  zero**: no baseline, no coverage or no authority yields no flag and
  an unavailable record with its reason — the gap-open and
  zero-baseline names must stay visible as the failure they are,
  never silently scored (reconcile §5–§6; U8). Trade-less seconds
  follow the sealed series construction bound at the seal; missing
  samples never become zeros inside an adapter (detector contract).
- **Latency and recall** are as defined in §K3.2. The percentile
  convention is stated with every table; the screen's two conventions
  moved no verdict, and the sealed harness names its own once
  (§K3.12), before results.

## K3.7 Costs (frozen: none consumed)

## K3.9 Dependence, replication, multiplicity; metrics and tests

- **Dependence:** symbol-sessions inside one trading date share a
  market day and are not independent replications; the trading date
  is the block (ADR-0019's whole-session block principle). Per-session
  tables come first; pooled figures carry their session count.
  Segment, feed and baseline-availability strata are descriptive,
  not independent looks.
- **Replication:** one session (2026-11-09) yields a development
  table only. Any stability claim needs a pre-registered replication
  over at least 5 sessions with walk-forward/CPCV discipline per
  ADR-0019 (U11); merit floors (≥30 round trips over ≥5 sessions)
  belong to the later return protocol, not here. Negative or
  embarrassing tables are recorded and kept, never re-run until
  positive.
- **Multiplicity:** one confirmatory look per sealed run. The family
  is every arm × condition read together; any interval or test
  attached at analysis time must be sourced and named at the seal
  (ADR-0019 names Romano–Wolf StepM across a family and Bonferroni
  for further preregistered looks) or reported UNAVAILABLE — never
  attached post hoc. Paired bootstrap intervals over whole-session
  blocks, if used, are named at the seal with their source. No
  best-arm selection after looking: choosing a favourite arm,
  sensitivity or subset after results is a new selection and a new
  protocol version. There is no peeking: outcomes are read once,
  after the seal and after trial registration (§K3.10).
- **Metrics (frozen list):** per arm and session — detected and
  censored counts; recall at onset; p50/p90 latency with the named
  percentile convention; ±60/±120/±300 s counts; false flags per
  symbol-hour (never-crossed and all-control separately); per-name
  flag counts; baseline-available share at onset; arm (c) persistence
  (in top 100 at onset among names that entered before onset); and
  the paired K2 contrasts on the identical names. DSR/PBO bind only
  when a later protocol selects among arms on returns; they are
  recorded not applicable to this detection measurement itself, with
  these trials inside that later count.

## K3.11 Decision rule (frozen: HOLD)

The sealed runs produce the §K3.12 tables and stop. The verdict
stays **HOLD** unless *all* of the following hold for an arm: a cited
mechanism (§K3.5); pre-declared parameters (§K3.8); a paired
lower-bound improvement over K2 on the same names under §K3.9's named
method; acceptable selectivity on the one shared control list; the
§K3.9 multi-session replication; and, for any production or K3
claim, the separate return-based ADR-0019 adoption gate (§K3.7).
Specifically: no detection recall number adopts the K3 composite,
calibrates a threshold, creates a registry default, or licenses sim,
paper or live use. Page's mechanism remains adopted *as a cited
research mechanism only*, exactly as the reconcile left it.

**The owner-exception question, stated and left open.** ADR-0003's K3

## 2. The score under test (frozen)

The unit under test is exactly the carried K2 implementation at the sealed
implementation SHA: `src/sextant/ranking/engine.py` (`advance_mover`,
`rank_movers`), `baseline.py` (`BaselineTable.cumulative`) and
`registry.py` (`Registry`, `ROWS`), themselves carries of
northstar-lanes@`00203275944a22655b3e14b221cfde9a265d549a` `mover.py`,
`baseline.py`, `trade_window.py`, `registry.py` and `contracts.py` (see the
ranking note and manifest row "Fixture ranking, generations and freshness
refusal"). If the sealed code differs from the formulas below, the code at
the sealed SHA governs and the discrepancy stops the run; the formulas are
written here so that check is possible. All money arithmetic is `Decimal`;
no float enters the score.

For one symbol in one feed (`sip` daytime, `boats` overnight, never pooled)
and one segment (PRE, RTH, POST, OVERNIGHT; §3), re-evaluated on every
eligible trade:

- `move = last_price / prior_close − 1`, where `last_price` is the most
  recent price-eligible trade price at or before the current trade and
  `prior_close` is the most recently completed official regular-session
  close, split-adjusted, on the same adjustment basis as `last_price`:
  POST uses that day's close, OVERNIGHT the session just ended, PRE/RTH
  the previous session (ADR-0003 K2). An undocumented reference gives
  `None`; there is no fallback close. The RTH open-based move is recorded
  separately and never enters the score.
- Segment dollar volume is the sum of `price × size` over volume-eligible
  trades from the segment start. It is defined only with a covered prefix:
  a late attachment without a verified prefix leaves the ratio unknown for
  the whole segment (`segment_prefix_unknown`). The actual closing print
  belongs to no extended segment, and closing-cross conditions update
  neither price nor volume history, exactly as the engine refuses them.
- The baseline normal at elapsed offset `s` seconds into the segment is the
  median, across prior sessions only, of each prior session's own
  cumulative dollar-volume curve read at the same offset (a median of
  per-session values, never a full-session total and never a difference of
  medians for cumulative queries). Within-minute bar curves are linearly
  interpolated and labelled as that approximation; offsets outside a
  curve's coverage contribute nothing (no zero, no extrapolation). A
  baseline containing the current or a future session is refused.
- `dvol_ratio = segment_dollar_volume / normal_dollar_volume`, defined
  only when the prefix is covered and the normal exists and is > 0.
- `trades_accel = recent_trades × baseline_seconds / (prior_trades ×
  recent_seconds)` over the registry windows below, defined only when the
  prior window is covered and non-zero.
- `score = move × dvol_ratio`. Rank is over **all computable rows**
  (score and acceleration both known), descending score, then descending
  `trades_accel`, then symbol ascending: a strict total order, rank 1
  first. Dollar volume, share volume, price, trade rate and spread never
  form the score and no price or ADV floor gates ranking (ADR-0003 K1/K2).

The four K2 gates — `move ≥ mover_min_move`, `move_1m > 0`,
`trades_accel ≥ mover_min_trades_accel`, `dvol_ratio ≥
mover_min_dvol_ratio` — are entry-qualification flags, not ranking floors:
excluded entries still rank with flags (ADR-0003 K2). The primary endpoints
in §5 therefore rank all computable rows. A secondary, predeclared table
repeats recall restricted to gate-qualified rows; it is descriptive only
and cannot replace the primary table after results are seen.

Every tunable is one of the twelve registry rows frozen by name and unit in
`registry.py` `ROWS` (`mover_min_move`, `mover_min_trades_accel`,
`mover_min_dvol_ratio`, `move_1m_window_s`, `move_5m_window_s`,
`trades_accel_window_s`, `trades_accel_baseline_min`,
`publish_min_interval_s`, `generation_max_age_s`, `heartbeat_max_age_s`,
`input_max_age_s`, `top_n`). This protocol freezes no numeric value for
them: values, sources, calibration states and last-change dates arrive as
reviewed registry rows supplied by the consuming runtime between sessions
(ADR-0018 L3) and are bound at the seal by `params_hash`. The values in
`tests/proofs/test_ranking.py` are fixture-only and are explicitly **not**
adopted (ranking note: "No operational values or authority are supplied
here"). All rows remain UNCALIBRATED; an uncalibrated starting value is
never measured success. If no reviewed rows exist at seal time, the
evaluation stays NOT_RUN. The number of prior sessions in each baseline
curve set is likewise a seal-bound receipt (§9), with no default here.

keeps its separate BOATS source identity and denominators; its quota and

result carries that label.

membership: every active NMS-listed common stock on Tapes A, B and C,

**Seed boundary.** The owner's package (957 selected gainers, 215
selected losers; weekly leaders 2021–2023 kept distinct from daily

historical inputs cannot supply the engine's price/volume eligibility

is NOT_ESTIMABLE for the affected quantity — masks are never guessed.

missing a required input (reference close, baseline, membership,

uses the row.

## 4. Truth and onset: hindsight labels, never features (frozen)

Truth and onset are computed from the session's own recorded price path
after the fact, for labelling only. No truth or onset quantity may enter
any feature, rank, gate or baseline. Both are defined from price versus
the official prior close alone, so neither can be circular with the K2
score, its gates, or its ranks.

- **Session move path.** For a symbol-session (one PIT identity on one
  trading date, overnight assigned as in §3), let the move at each
  price-eligible trade be `price / prior_close − 1` on the §2 basis.
  `truth_move` is the maximum of that path over the trading date, with
  the feed and segment of the maximizing trade retained. Feeds are never
  mixed inside one ranking generation (§2), but the hindsight maximum
  may be attained in any segment; its segment and feed labels say where.
- **Truth mover (primary).** A symbol-session with `truth_move ≥ 0.05`
  (Decimal, inclusive). The 5% line is the pinned missed-movers
  witness's mover threshold (its table's +5% column and "11 observed
  +5% movers", carried in the extreme-mover evidence note), adopted
  here as an UNCALIBRATED evaluation label — it is not an entry
  threshold, not the registry's `mover_min_move`, and not evidence that
  5% is economically meaningful. The +10% crossing is recorded
  descriptively in the same witness's shape; it creates no second truth
  set and no additional endpoint family.
- **Onset (primary anchor).** For a truth mover, onset is the first
  price-eligible trade, in event-time order within its feed and
  segment, at which the causal move first reaches `≥ 0.05`. Its
  evaluation time `τ` is that trade's known-at time as recorded
  (`max(ts_event, ts_init)` on the trade, consistent with the engine's
  `known_at`); for bar-fidelity sessions the onset is known only at bar
  close, and `τ` is the bar's close clock, labelled as such (the
  witness's "minute starts, known only at minute end" caveat, frozen).
  Onset is therefore the hindsight moment the move had provably begun
  in size, against which "ranked as it starts" can be judged without
  consulting any detector.
- **What onset is not.** This is not the K3 surge/onset detector
  (anomalous short-window dollar volume, minimum prints, move off the
  quiet price, rising at the decision, first leg within
  `first_leg_window_s`; ADR-0003 K3, ADR-0024). That detector's
  experiment is NOT_RUN and its upstream onset convergence is
  REDESIGN-NEED (candidate-history audit); this protocol builds no
  detector, fits no quiet price, and evaluates no K3 threshold. Event
  time, first moved print, detector flag and known-at remain distinct
  clocks (ADR-0024); only the price-path anchor above is frozen here.
  A future detector evaluation needs its own sourced protocol.
- **Recorded per mover** (the witness table's shape, its cohort not
  reused): PIT identity and symbol, prior close with source, first
  trade time, +5% onset time and `τ`, +10% time or its absence,
  `truth_move` with its segment/feed, first top-30 and first top-100
  entry times (§5), rank at onset, and every unknown reason. A trading
  date with no truth mover has undefined recall (§5): it is counted and
  reported as a no-truth session, never scored 0 and never dropped.

## 5. Generations and recall@30/100 per session (frozen)

**What is ranked, when.** Scores recompute on every eligible trade (§2).
The evaluation reads ranking **generations** — what a consumer could
actually know — published at most once per `publish_min_interval_s`,
each carrying identity, coverage and known-at time. A stale, invalid or
incomplete generation is refused with no fallback to an earlier good one
(ADR-0003 K6); the separately tagged top-N projection is incomplete for
consumption and is never scored in place of the full generation
(ranking note). The rank current at time `t` in a feed/segment is the
rank in the latest valid full generation of that feed/segment with
known-at `≤ t` (inclusive). If no such generation exists, the symbol is
unranked at `t`. Ranks from different feeds or segments are never merged
into one list.

**Endpoints.** For `k ∈ {30, 100}` — the house recall depths already in
use (the saved-scanner witness reports @30 and @100; the design records
6/41 and 25/41 at its earliest publication under its own, different
truth, which this protocol does not reuse):

- `hit@k(m)`: truth mover `m` has rank `≤ k` (inclusive) in the
  generation current at its onset time `τ(m)`, in its onset feed and
  segment. A mover unranked at `τ` — including score-unknown for any
  recorded reason — is a miss, with the reason retained.
- **Primary:** per trading date `d` with truth set `T(d)`,
  `recall@k(d) = |{m ∈ T(d) : hit@k(m)}| / |T(d)|`, reported as the
  count pair (hits, `|T(d)|`) beside the ratio. Undefined when
  `|T(d)| = 0` (§4). This onset-anchored, per-session pair at both
  depths is the evaluation's primary result.
- **Timing (predeclared, same trial):** per mover and depth, the first
  generation time at or after its onset-segment start, in its onset
  feed and segment, at which it enters the
  top `k`, and the signed lead `τ(m) − first-entry` (positive: ranked
  before onset size was reached; negative: late; absent: never entered,
  a censored miss, never an infinite or imputed lead). Reported as
  counts and quantiles over observed leads with the censored count
  beside them, per segment and overall.
- **Trajectory (predeclared, descriptive):** per trading date and
  depth, recall recomputed with every mover judged in the generation
  current at each segment's close (that segment's last valid generation
  at or before segment end). This shows when recall arrives; it cannot
  substitute for the onset-anchored primary after results are seen.
- Top `k` when fewer than `k` rows are computable is all computable
  rows; the computable-universe size is reported with every recall so a
  shallow universe cannot flatter the ratio invisibly.

**Aggregation and dependence.** Per-session results come first (the W2
row's "per session"); pooled micro-average (summed hits over summed
truth) and session macro-average (mean of per-session ratios over
sessions with defined recall) are both reported, neither hidden, with
session counts. Symbol-sessions inside one trading date share a market
day and are not independent replications; the trading date is the block
(ADR-0019's whole-session block principle), and segment, feed,
price-band and year breakdowns are descriptive strata, not independent
looks. This protocol freezes counts and ratios as its confirmatory
output and claims no interval: no sourced interval method for recall
proportions exists in the repo, so any interval must be sourced and
named at the seal or reported UNAVAILABLE — never attached post hoc.
Walk-forward, CPCV, DSR and PBO are **not applicable** to this
single-arm descriptive recall measurement (there is no fitted choice,
no return series and no selection to deflate) and are recorded as such;
they bind in full, with this trial inside the cumulative count, the
moment any later protocol selects among scores, thresholds or arms
using this cohort (§7). Costs, fills and P&L play no part: recall is a
pre-economic ranking measurement, and no cost model is frozen because
none is consumed; any economic claim needs a separate protocol with
ADR-0019's costed, paired machinery.

## 6. Matched non-mover controls (frozen)

Controls answer one question: at the moments movers reached onset, how
much of the top 30/100 would comparable non-movers have occupied
anyway? Without that base rate, recall alone cannot distinguish ranking
signal from a hot tape.

- **Control pool.** Same trading date, same PIT universe (§3),
  symbol-sessions that are not truth movers (`truth_move < 0.05`) and
  that appear as a row (computable or score-unknown, with reasons) in
  the generation judging their matched mover. No-trade symbol-sessions
  have no row and enter neither numerator nor denominator; they are
  counted separately so the exclusion stays visible. Seed losers, seed
  absence and scanner-archive membership never define controls
  (ADR-0027, §3).
- **Matching (exact-cell, knowable-only).** Each truth mover is matched
  on variables knowable at its onset `τ`: trading date (exact), onset
  segment (exact), onset feed (exact), and ADR-0003 K7 price band of
  the prior close (≥ $5, $1–$5, < $1; boundaries: $5 belongs to the top
  band, $1 to the middle band, exactly as the labels read). The
  mover's control set is **every** eligible non-mover in its cell —
  full-cell controls, no sampling, no seed, no ratio to tune. A mover
  whose cell contains no eligible non-mover is reported unmatched; its
  cell is never widened after results are seen. Labels stratify and
  never gate ranking itself (ADR-0003 K7/K8); cap bucket stays unused
  while point-in-time shares are null.
- **Control endpoint.** In the same generation that judges the mover's
  `hit@k` (§5), the control occupancy rate is the fraction of its
  matched controls with rank `≤ k`; unranked controls count as not in
  the top `k` with reasons retained. The predeclared contrast, per
  trading date and depth, is `recall@k(d)` beside the mean matched
  control occupancy over that date's matched movers, with mover,
  matched and unmatched counts. It is descriptive: no significance
  claim is frozen (§5), and a favourable contrast is not an adoption
  gate.
- **Named limit.** No sourced distance, propensity or optimal-matching
  method exists in this repo, and the candidate-history audit records
  the frozen ordinary-control protocol as a blocker precisely because
  Rule 1 stops unsupported control construction. This protocol
  therefore freezes only exact stratification on sourced labels,
  claims no causal matching and no covariate balance beyond its four
  exact factors, and leaves richer matching to a future protocol that
  brings its own pinned method. If even these labels cannot be
  supplied point-in-time for a session, that session reports recall
  with controls UNAVAILABLE rather than controls improvised.

## 8. Decision rule (frozen: measurement only)

The sealed run produces the §10 tables and stops. No value of recall,
lead or control contrast adopts, calibrates, promotes or retires
anything: K2 remains the incumbent house score, UNCALIBRATED, exactly
as before the run, whatever the tables say. A negative or embarrassing
result is recorded and kept and is never re-run until it turns positive
(research lane rule). Any proposal that follows — a calibrated
threshold, a variant comparison, an entry claim, a sim or paper step —
starts from its own preregistration citing this result, carries this
trial in its cumulative count, and meets ADR-0019's paired,
stressed-cost adoption machinery there, not here. Paper operation is
neither gated nor licensed by this measurement.
