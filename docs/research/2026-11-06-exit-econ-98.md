# Our exit engine against holding, on #41's exact inputs (#98)

**Result: our exit engine loses to holding.** At its recorded paper starts, Sextant's exit engine (#45) returned **−49.71/−53.52 bps per trade** on the same 1,025 entries where holding to 15:55 returned **+76.44/+70.42** (3/6 bps per side). It also loses to the old study's selected variant on the same tape (+35.43/+29.62).

Nearly all of the gap comes from pre-arm exits: the 1% buffer, hit by minute lows that then recover. This is a minute-bar approximation, MODELED, at uncalibrated starts. It does not test calibrated values, sub-minute execution or re-entry.

## Arms
All arms use n = 1,025 (the primary complete-horizon entries, October 26–28) and the same inputs, entries and tape. The trial rows are in [trials.jsonl](2026-11-06-exit-econ-98/trials.jsonl).

| Arm | 3 bps | 6 bps |
|---|---:|---:|
| Hold to 15:55 (equal to #67) | 76.44 | 70.42 |
| C5-n22-k3-R-OFF, study's fill model (equal to #67) | 54.78 | 48.76 |
| C5-n22-k3-R-OFF, filled at the signal bar's proxy bid | 35.43 | 29.62 |
| **Exit engine at starts, R_w dropped** | **−49.71** | **−53.52** |
| Exit engine at starts, R_w strict | 76.44 | 70.42 |

- **R_w strict:** no trigger is ever set on minute bars, so this arm never sells; it equals hold by construction.
- **C5 on our tape** costs about 19 bps against its own fill model. That cost is the proxy's pessimism: it fills at the signal bar's low, not the next open.

## Where the gain went
For the engine with R_w dropped, at 3 bps per side (6 bps in [report.json](2026-11-06-exit-econ-98/report.json)). Each exit's label is the event that ended it. The gap columns sum to the whole −126.15 bps gap.

| Exit | n | Engine mean | Hold mean, same entries | Share of the gap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pre-arm breach (trigger `entry − buffer`) | 388 | −154.16 | +142.41 | **−112.26** |
| Gain-lock breach (armed) | 374 | +31.34 | +69.41 | −13.89 |
| Flat at 15:55 (never breached) | 263 | −10.88 | −10.88 | 0.00 |

- **Pre-arm exits sold recoveries.** 233 of the 388 entries would have ended positive at 15:55. Engine median −107.35 bps, hold median +76.40. The persisted breach of three minute lows at or below `entry − max(tick, spread, 0.01·entry)` fired on dips that recovered. ADR-0005 accepts that "a tight trigger sells more often on noise"; lanes X2–X7 expects re-entry (ADR-0006, design row 9) to recover the move. Re-entry is not in this arm.
- **Gain-lock exits gave back about half the day's move:** median +13.82 against hold's +26.62.
- **Take-profit: 0 touches.** No held minute rose more than 6.5% above its prior high (maximum 1.0651), and touching the 10% take-profit needs a 10% jump. So the roll never bound here.
- **Chase:** off. Its interval has no value (ruling B1), so no exit was chased.

## Method
- **Inputs, cohort and controls:** #67's catalog on the hash-checked inputs, with the pinned study's own `opening_entries`, `replay` and `net_return`. Both controls equal #67's committed rows.
- **Tape:** the pinned minute-bar proxy, northstar-trading@8256c16c `research_e2e/backtests/b_gain_protection.py:856–905`, per ruling B2. At each completed minute: a quote at the bar low with bid/ask = low × (1 ∓ h) on the broker tick (bid floored, ask ceiled; ticks from :411–415), then a trade at the bar high, then the bar. h is the study's own cost per side.
- **Entry:** the same file's anchor-and-entry pattern (:705–735). The engine's fill equals the recorded fill on every entry, or the run stops.
- **Engine:** `sextant.exits` at `ExitParams.starts()`:
  - from trading ADR-0019 at `7c5f852a`: buffer 0.01, range multiple 1 over 10 s, giveback 0.1, breach persisting 3 quotes over 1 s, backstop separation 0.05;
  - take-profit 0.10/0.02 (ADR-0005 X5);
  - quote age 1 s (lanes `parameters.py`);
  - chase cap 0.01, *implied by ADR-0019:296 (trading@7c5f852a), not a recorded start*;
  - `c_RT` = the study's round-trip cost.
- **Costs:** the entry and a 15:55 flat pay the study's cost per side, as in its `net_return`. A proxy-bid exit already embeds h and is not charged again.
- **Ledger:** every arm, including R_w strict, is a MODELED trial row in #67's shape. Nothing is selected.

## Not estimable on minute bars, by ruling (#98)
- **Chase** (B1): `chase_min_interval_s` has no value, so the chase is off.
- **R_w** (B3, pending): the 10 s range window holds no proxy trade, so both treatments are reported.
- **Persistence:** 3 quotes over 1 s becomes 3 consecutive minute lows, a slower test than the ADR's.
