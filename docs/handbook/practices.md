# Practices

*The working practices that survived the harvest. Each line names the record it comes from; a practice with no source is dropped.*

## Rank

- Publish a full-market ranking generation at most once per `publish_min_interval_s`; a missing, stale or invalid generation means no entry, with no fallback. [ADR-0003](../adr/0003-ranking-and-feeds.md)
- Score `move × dvol_ratio` against the symbol's own time-matched segment baseline, ties by `trades_accel`; dollar volume, share count, price and trade rate never form the score. [ADR-0003](../adr/0003-ranking-and-feeds.md)
- Measure the move from the latest completed, split-adjusted official regular-session close, and record the RTH open-based move separately. [ADR-0003](../adr/0003-ranking-and-feeds.md)
- Keep SIP and BOATS denominators, source identity and coverage separate; an unknown coverage is unknown, not zero. [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-us-equity-data-stack.md)
- Carry latency labels — publication, vendor receipt, local receipt, generation, entry-eligible, submission — on every ranking record. [ADR-0003](../adr/0003-ranking-and-feeds.md)

## Enter

- Enter the first leg: a non-decreasing run of eligible trades inside `first_leg_window_s`, no more than `first_leg_max_move` above the quiet price; a lower trade restarts the run. [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-entry.md) Apply the [rank veto at the native Strategy decision](rank-gate.md), reading the current switch at that seam with the same-call consumer result, raw symbol and current clock; its OFF registry row preserves the original decision bytes.
- A missing ATR or VWAP makes extension UNKNOWN, never "not extended"; a fresh spike high is never the entry. [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-entry.md)
- Freeze the buying-power basis at segment start with the segment identity; no read of buying power means no entry. [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-entry.md)
- Send LIMIT at the ask, `extended_hours` outside RTH with `time_in_force` `day` or `gtc`, reprice at most `entry_chase_bps`, cancel at `entry_ttl_s`, never market. [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-entry.md)
- Size from planned risk, not from conviction: `stop_distance = entry − initial_trigger + chase_cap_fraction × entry`, bounded by fresh buying power and a flow-share cap. [ADR-0004](../adr/0004-entry.md), [ADR-0011](../adr/0011-sizing-and-auto-leverage.md)
- Keep mover slots `ceil(0.4 × cap)` and hold the top `3 ×` quota in the candidate pool. Use configured capacity, never candidate-list length; retained names charge their own pool, potential fills before movers, then unused places pass both ways with potential first. Preserve published ranking through qualification: first-moved time is evidence, not extra priority. Runtime callers supply held/working symbol sets, never just a count or a fabricated ranking timestamp; session capacity is not the mover reservation. Validate changed APIs against callers on freshly merged gate, not only the branch's original base. Prove fractional quotas (3 of 7), capacity with excess candidates, and retained-potential pass-over, not just integral examples (2 of 5, 8 of 20). [ADR-0024](../adr/0024-extreme-mover-ranking-and-early-entry.md); northstar-trading@8256c16c31dcb8e53fc726e0c1c77dc2c8d0d4fa:src/northstar_trading/strategies/premarket_gap/scanner.py:991-1105; #187 F1/F2/F3, #201 merged-tree C integration

## Protect

- The trigger starts below entry and only rises; keep the setup gap until arming and never rebase an adopted position to the mark. [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-exit-engine.md)
- Every in-gain holding gets broker-held protection that follows the gain — a replaced OCO/bracket stop or one native trailing stop — including reconciled owner fills on B; this is the required policy, not proof of continuous installed protection. B retains its separate cancel-first policy and handover gaps. [ADR-0025](../adr/0025-gain-protection-and-fast-exit.md)
- Arm only on a completed confirmation at `entry + c_RT + noise_floor` with an executable price strictly above the armed trigger; an unknown cost basis, custody or quote stays UNARMED. [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-exit-engine.md)
- Never sell on one quote, print or bar: a breach needs `breach_persist_count` observations over `breach_persist_span_s`. [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-exit-engine.md)
- Chase with one managed LIMIT at `max(bid, last × (1 − chase_cap_fraction))`, at most once per `chase_min_interval_s`, serialized; an acknowledgement is not a fill. [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-exit-engine.md)
- Raise the native backstop in place, never lower it; a DAY order does not flatten inventory, and an outage freezes the backstop at its last acknowledgement. [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-exit-engine.md), [ADR-0020](../adr/0020-live-lane.md)

## Re-enter

- Wait for a flat receipt: broker net quantity zero plus terminal lineage for the entry and every protection. [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-reentry-and-rotation.md)
- Retry the same symbol inside `retry_rank_band`, rotate to a better unheld name inside `rotation_rank_band`, otherwise wait. [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-reentry-and-rotation.md)
- Buy a reclaim only on a pullback, a held higher low and a hold, at most `reclaim_max_above_buffers` buffers above the level; never the first print. [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-reentry-and-rotation.md)
- Rotate a held name only when `expected_lift > 2 × c_RT ÷ price + rotation_lift_margin_bps ÷ 10,000`. [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-reentry-and-rotation.md)
- Paper retries are unbounded and ungated; live is `live_nonrenewing` at 1× with retry and rotation OFF until a separate promotion. [ADR-0006](../adr/0006-reentry-and-rotation.md), [ADR-0020](../adr/0020-live-lane.md)

## Custody and budgets

- The owner's orders are never cancelled, sold back, resized or adopted; every unreserved whole long share is covered by exactly one house trail of the full net quantity, re-armed whenever that quantity changes, and pages are informational. [ADR-0007](../adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md)
- One writer and one pacer per account, one exit per quantity, never a second full-size exit; a foreign order or a quantity mismatch freezes the symbol and pages. [ADR-0007](../adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md)
- Recheck paper submission admission after durable registration, excluding only that request's own reservation; a refusal retains lineage and emits no native command or wire accounting. [ADR-0007](../adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md), [shared-node regression](../research/2026-11-06-shared-paper-node.md)
- SELLs require row-8 custody readiness and enough unreserved residual before and after registration; an account callback cannot grant missing custody or bypass an unreported exit. [ADR-0007](../adr/0007-custody-and-order-writing.md), [flatten witness](../research/2026-11-06-shared-paper-node.md)
- Refuse known native validation before accounting without an account halt; once the accounting hook starts, errors retain the unknown-outcome halt. Unexpected and admission errors still halt. [ADR-0009](../adr/0009-order-rate-limits.md), [native boundary witness](../research/2026-11-06-shared-paper-node.md)
- Pace every wire action with priority CANCEL > REDUCE > MODIFY > QUERY > SUBMIT, reserve protection capacity per open and exiting position, and back off on 429. [ADR-0009](../adr/0009-order-rate-limits.md)
- Use about 80% of a lane's action budget over a session, run protection first, and investigate a 15-minute zero-action session rather than forcing a trade. [ADR-0002](../adr/0002-philosophy-and-lanes.md)
- Paper observes the guard; live enforces it, and an unknown gate identifier enforces and logs an error. [ADR-0010](../adr/0010-risk-guard-pacing-and-halt.md)
- Validate the exact code an adapter sends: derive the native account and port from the same account object that keys the writer lease, check every identifier grammar the pinned adapter accepts (composite IDs, generic spreads) at its cited parser paths, and prove a forbidden environment fallback with a valid environment identity and a blank explicit input. Keep one registry authority per shared limit, parse offline inventory in a committed proof, and pass explicit, sourced data-policy starts to the native config. ([#93](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/pull/93)) Keep native engine clients on their owner loop: worker payloads and callbacks carry weak references, resolved only on that loop; cancel and drain before clearing retained handlers and completed-task tracebacks. Executor completion alone does not acknowledge a queued readiness callback: await that future. Parse status rows inside the row boundary, before SDK model construction can escape it; scope codes by tape, and publish native unknown with nullable trading state for unrepresented codes instead of retaining a prior trading indication. [Status sources and native-shell proof](../manifest.md) (`test_alpaca_status.py`, 2026-11-10). Keep correction/cancel parsing before SDK casts too: preserve wire nanoseconds with MessagePack's integer API and Decimal prices/sizes from raw fields; global SDK handlers must follow current named trade memberships across later subscriptions. [Pinned amendment sources and bus proofs](../manifest.md) (`test_alpaca_trade_updates.py`, 2026-11-11). [Pinned AX shutdown and Python runtime source](../manifest.md) (`test_alpaca_data.py` ownership and startup proofs; 2026-11-10 flaky-proof lesson).

## Sessions

- Follow the XNYS calendar with actual early closes; outside regular hours every order is LIMIT-only, and A's tiers trade at half risk. [ADR-0002](../adr/0002-philosophy-and-lanes.md)
- Paper runs 24/5 and carries no conservative profile: each arm runs the full factor stack, every factor set has a paired with/without arm, and paper P&L alone still decides nothing. Replay and evaluation run 24/7, with closed markets used for replay, stress, reflection and proposals. [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md) Read asset listings with the pinned SDK's `raw_data` option and validate selector fields per row: a new vendor enum must skip and count that row, with one run warning and a receipt count, while valid peers remain usable. Keep account/order responses typed. [Pinned SDK and regression proofs](../manifest.md) (C universe listing lesson, 2026-11-10).
- Ask which clock a number came from — historical minute bars end at start + 1 minute, daily bars at the next New York midnight, live bars at receipt — and never clamp a signed skew. [ADR-0013](../adr/0013-us-equity-data-stack.md)

## Evidence and learning

- Reconcile selected seeds without changing their weekly/daily selection, raw missingness or dated tickers. An unrepresented session is not a negative, selected losers are not ordinary controls, and unknown availability or cumulative trials remain null. [ADR-0027](../adr/0027-extreme-gainer-research-package.md); [lesson and witness](candidate-history.md)
- Every exit leaves its counterfactual: hold-to-now, matched hold-flat, managed policy, managed plus re-entry, and each shadow arm, with n, censoring and costs at 1× and stressed. [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md)
- Each tunable value has a registry row and source. A sourced value without calibration is an uncalibrated starting value, never measured success. If the value or its authority is missing, keep it unavailable; fixtures supply no default authority. Reviewed changes occur between sessions, and nightly tuning only proposes. [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md) B's quote simulator refuses missing chase rows before adoption. [B witness](../research/2026-11-06-owner-b-manager.md)
- Separate source-component parity from admission: a posterior is not an alarm or a completed-window screen a rank/first-second trigger. Runtime mappings govern behavior and hashes; carry coverage/source evidence through each window and bind interpreted paper conventions. [#130 source/admission contract](../../lanes/research/detector-adapters.md); [review lesson and witnesses](detector-components.md)
- One cumulative trial ledger, tiers never pooled, history never reset, rejected variants kept. Publish the durable trial before its receipt; retain per-symbol refusal reasons across segment changes and identify a waiting local trial file by absolute path and SHA-256 until the sole ledger writer ingests it. [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md); [TrialLog source](../research/2026-11-06-ledger-merit.md)
- Adoption needs a dependence-aware lower bound above the incumbent and above hold-flat at stressed costs; otherwise keep the incumbent and record the negative. [ADR-0019](../adr/0019-validation-and-merit.md)
- Merit is ≥30 independent round trips over ≥5 sessions at PSR ≥ 0.95 with replay correlation ≥0.9 and slippage ≤1.5× modelled; it produces a draft, never a promotion. [ADR-0019](../adr/0019-validation-and-merit.md)
- Nightly reflection and tuning write proposals only; a label or a one-day anecdote never adopts a change. A nightly proposal PR carries no registry patch and never calls the registry's reviewed-write path; adoption is a separate change in the consuming runtime. Manual Orca runs skip the scheduler's precheck, so readiness is checked at run entry too. [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md), [L4 design](../research/2026-11-06-nightly-proposals.md) ([#143](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/pull/143))
- Read outcomes only as of the cutoff; keep superseded and negative lessons. Historical collection keeps original response texts, feed/window identity and empty windows; a data receipt never qualifies a width or substitutes for a trial-ledger run. [ADR-0018](../adr/0018-evidence-and-learning-loop.md); [calibration job](../../deploy/calibration/README.md), D-44/LEAD-2127 §5

## Harness

- Discovery is capability inventory, not authority: the official TradingView MCP's 35-tool inventory must not widen the five-tool membership adapter. Re-read the vendor documentation on every TradingView PR and keep unknown tools refused before any call. [Official MCP](https://www.tradingview.com/mcp/docs), read 2026-11-09; [dated out-list and offline drift proof](../tradingview.md#official-mcp-drift-fence); [ADR-0021](../adr/0021-tradingview-display.md).
- One manifest row per pick or practice, with pin, application and `just prove` command; nothing enters without one and a stopped practice is deleted. When merging parallel lane work, retain main's rows and append the lane's new rows at the end of their existing section. Reusing a pin that main already carries is not a new pick. Only a new pick waits `pins.json`'s `release_cooldown_days` (lead ruling 2026-11-09). [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-sextant-foundation.md); lead direction for [PR #70](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/pull/70), 2026-11-06; [merge lesson](retrieval.md)
- A PR lands through squash auto-merge only when ci, source and review pass. `ci` runs on `pull_request` on GitHub-hosted runners with no secrets. One `workflow_run` gate from the default branch then posts `source` (Danger, API-only) and `review` (dsh through PerryLink/dsh-github; the API-key reviewers run only for a dsh-authored PR, a risk-path PR, or a gate-path PR, which gets one extra second-family review in place of an owner label, by the owner's 2026-11-09 decision; a Codex author adds one only when it selects Claude (D-34, 2026-11-10)). So a PR cannot change its own checks. Review to Google's standard: approve a PR that definitely improves code health even if it isn't perfect, and let facts and data overrule opinion. A finding blocks only as an Always-P0 item, or as a hard-line, custody, money or correctness defect with a failing witness at the exact head. Every blocking finding states its evidence status: an Always-P0 item may block with the label `static: no witness run`, and any other blocking finding needs an executed witness at the exact head — the command or test run and the output it produced ([review-rules.md line 27](../../.github/workflows/shared/review-rules.md#report)). Everything else goes to one follow-up issue per PR. Keep one concern per PR. At most two reviewed commits: if a blocking class recurs, split the PR or ship that path disabled and failing closed, and when a bug class recurs, simplify the design instead of patching instances. After a third failed fix round on one layer, a state table of that layer and a line map against the official source may help: on 2026-11-11 the E5 client's r5 round wrote both, and they found cases the reviews then confirmed ([#569](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant/pull/569)). A branch-only `just ci` pass does not validate CI's merged tree: retain both branches' registered proofs when resolving conflicts, then test the combined tree. Before turning auto-merge on, check the size budgets on current `main` plus the PR, because a green ci on a stale `refs/pull/N/merge` can still break `main`. [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-sextant-foundation.md); [Google eng-practices](https://github.com/google/eng-practices/blob/3bb3ec25b3b0199f4940b1aa75f0ac5c5753301c/review/reviewer/standard.md) `standard.md:31-32,72,90`, `small-cls.md`, `looking-for.md:46-57`; [Release Toggles](https://martinfowler.com/articles/feature-toggles.html), read 2026-11-09 ([#101](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/pull/101), [#164](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/pull/164)); [landing verdict rule](../../.github/workflows/shared/review-rules.md#report); the static gate reviews of [#549](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant/pull/549)
- Converge, don't collide: a setup step edits its target in place rather than skipping once it exists ([#55](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/pull/55)); configure by directory and marker, never a shared list that every lane appends to ([#92](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/pull/92)); a memory floor reads MemAvailable, not "free"; write a post body with a quoted heredoc (`<<'EOF'`) and a `__STAMP__` that sed fills from a `TZ=America/New_York date` read taken just before the post — never a time typed ahead — because an unquoted heredoc runs backticked text; grep a regex-form deny rule by its pattern, not a literal word; and one shared GitHub token is one budget, so no polling loops, and GraphQL reads first ([issue #44](https://github.com/seathatflowsinourveins/sextant-archive/issues/44)).
- Keep always-loaded rules short and enforce boundaries instead of hooks; the only hooks are the secret scan and the skills runtime. [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-sextant-foundation.md)
- Agent sessions run locally. Checks run on GitHub-hosted runners, which are free on public repos. PR code runs only in `ci`, with a read-only token and no secrets, and fork PRs need approval and get no secrets. [ADR-0001](../adr/0001-sextant-foundation.md)
- After a lane worker hands off, AO is the branch's only writer for CI/review fixes. Claiming records ownership only: check out the PR head and confirm its SHA before editing; each writer turn takes ONE consolidated rule delivery from its dispatcher, and rulings never reach a running turn along a relay chain. Detect a stalled turn by its terminal's lastOutputAt (`orca terminal show`), and resume it with the runtime's own conversation id. [AO hand-off evidence](../research/2026-11-06-agent-orchestrator.md)
- Run harness proof subprocesses with explicit fixture environments; inherited Bash functions or startup files must not replace fake tools with the running service. [AO proof isolation](../research/2026-11-06-agent-orchestrator.md#offline-proof-isolation)
- Probe a replacement router key before publishing its sealed value; a failed probe keeps the previous sealed bytes and router key. Check both in the regression. Hermes's gateway and sessions keep their launch key, so relaunch its clients and restart its gateway before revoking. [Renewal lesson](omni-keys.md)
- Only the owner signs in, stores external keys and approves/applies live limits. The owner may halt at any time; the sanctioned guard and runner also halt automatically under their recorded causes, and the lead promotes, stages and demotes with a record. API keys are spent only where quality needs them. dsh reviews every PR; Codex or Claude review a dsh-authored or risk-path PR, a Codex author's only when it selects Claude (D-34, 2026-11-10); local Codex uses the OmniRoute pool and never OPENAI_API_KEY (owner, 2026-11-09).
